Proposal review

Review recognition sets by instructional unit and literature anchors by machine signature. Each decision is posed at the grain used by the later promotion step.

A verdict records a judgment. It changes no knowledge or curriculum file. Accepting a unit set does not promote it into explicit_lesson_strategy/4; promotion is a separate act.
How the queues are formed and ranked

Unit review combines the machines proposed across a unit and keeps the motivating lessons with each machine. Signature review retains signatures with at least one mechanically clear candidate. It labels score ties, along with displacement by an already-bound signature. Reuse of one row across several signatures has its own label. Defect labels set the first ranking tiers; score band ranks the remaining signatures.

Score is a lexical match produced by the proposal generator. In the assessment sample, every proposal at 4.5 or above was correct. Every proposal at 3.5 or below was wrong or could not be settled from the proposal. Treat these as sample bounds rather than acceptance rules.
