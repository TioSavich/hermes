What Hermes does with a lesson it cannot compute

Most of the lesson census asks whether an automaton computes a lesson's arithmetic. These 180 lessons of 226 ask for something else, and until this build they were counted only as an absence. Each card below carries a machine reading the lesson's structural form, running that form on inputs, and printing what it produced. 40 forms are in use across five lanes. 99 of 192 enactments are well formed; the rest print the reason they stopped short. 78 ran on values the machine supplied because the lesson asks a class to bring its own, and 4 ran on values the guide prints as one worked sample of an open task. Every one of those says so on its own card.

Read the last line of every card. A machine that sorts shapes has not watched a child sort shapes, and a machine that lists what a data display answers has not held a discussion. The forms are read from the curriculum and the steps are executed; the classroom is not. Nothing here is a coverage number for the arithmetic rungs, which are counted separately and are unaffected by anything on this page.

Built by scripts/enactment/build_best_im.py from data/learningcommons/derived/lesson_enactments/, which the enactors write by running. Scenes drawn through hermes/app/rendering.py; this page owns no drawing code. 72 of 192 enactments produced a drawing; the others printed a record.
