model: checking knowledge base: cold
Everything on this page runs on this machine; no network call leaves it. The model never receives student names unless they are pasted here. An abstention row in the panel means the knowledge base holds nothing for that ask, and the reply must say so. The first consultation starts the knowledge base and can take about eleven seconds.
