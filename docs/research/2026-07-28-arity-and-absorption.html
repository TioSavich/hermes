Arity and absorption

Why the error-rule corpus cannot earn an incompatibility entailment, and what it yields instead. Written for a reader who follows the dog-and-mammal example and stops there; the argument runs in five pictures.

Every count on this page was recomputed on 2026-07-28 from the tracked artifacts listed at the foot. The register quantifies over declared sets only, so it is exactly as strong as the closed world it ranges over.

The number that would not move

Hermes keeps a finite register (formal/incompatibility/incompatibility_entailment_order.pl) that models Brandom's incompatibility-entailment over declared data. Two coding waves put student error rules into it as incompatible triples: a fraction-comparison pilot (15 rule families), then a decimal slice (75 more). The register's emergent incompatible sets went from 4 to 94. Its earned strict entailments were 21 before, 21 after the pilot, and 21 after the decimal slice. Late today the count moved at last, 21 to 25, through a new shape of declared set (the closure of the final section) and through no additional triple.

A flat count beside a twentyfold rise ordinarily asks for more data. More data was the first diagnosis, and the decimal slice was chosen to supply exactly what that diagnosis asked for. The count still did not move, and the reason is structural: coded in this shape, the corpus cannot earn an entailment, and further coding in the same shape cannot change that. The rest of this page is that argument.

The machine, run on dog and mammal

The register's primitive is the declared incompatible set: a list of contents that cannot be held together. {dog, feline} is such a set, and a larger collection counts as incoherent when it contains a declared set. (The register keeps each declared set in smallest form, and it files contents that have no declared set at all under a separate, weaker heading, "vacuous"; nothing below concerns those.)

Entailment is defined the way Brandom defines it: dog entails mammal when everything incompatible with mammal is incompatible with dog. Over finite data that becomes a replacement test, which Figure 1 runs in full. Take each declared set through mammal, remove mammal, put dog in its place, and check whether the result still contains a declared set. If every replacement stays incoherent, and some replacement in the reverse direction does not, the register records that dog earns mammal, strictly.

It is tempting to credit the result to dog's excluding so much: everything mammal excludes and more. But the test does not run on amount. It runs on recurrence, plus an asymmetry. The replacement in Figure 1 stays incoherent because cold_blooded, the partner that mammal left behind, also has a declared set with dog; and the reverse fails because feline excludes dog and not mammal, a cat being both feline and mammal. A corpus in which dog stood in ten thousand declared sets, none of them sharing a partner with mammal's, would earn nothing at all.

What a third member changes

Call what is left of a set, once the tested content is removed, its remainder. In Figure 1 every set had two members, so each remainder was a single content, and a candidate passed by having a declared set whose one other member was that content. Figure 2 puts a two-member set and a three-member set side by side and runs the same step on each.

The word for what Figure 2 draws is absorption: a set through the candidate absorbs a remainder when all its other members sit inside that remainder. The whole test reduces to this. A earns B exactly when, for every declared set through B, some set through A absorbs the remainder. At two members, absorption needs one shared partner. At three members, it needs a recurring pair.

Every coded triple carries a private pair

Each student error rule enters the register as one three-member set: the rule, the consequence it licenses, and the context where the licence fails. For instance, "partition the dividend into divisor-many whole groups" licenses "the group size is the quotient" and fails where "the divisor is not a whole number". Measured over the corpus: 90 triples; 90 distinct rule contents, each in exactly one triple; 90 distinct licensed consequences, none in two; 76 distinct contexts, 8 of them shared by two to four triples. So every triple carries two contents that occur nowhere else in the entire relation. Figure 3 runs the test on one.

Puncture any triple at its rule and the remainder is the pair {its consequence, its context}. The consequence sits in one declared set only, the set just punctured, so no candidate has a set that absorbs, and the rule can neither earn nor be earned. Punctured at the consequence, the same holds by symmetry. Punctured at the context, the remainder is the private pair itself, which only its own triple contains. The measured result over all 256 error-rule contents: zero earned, zero equivalent. And since any triple coded in this shape brings its own private pair with it, the zero is invariant under corpus growth. Aggregation was never the lever.

The experiment that confirmed it

The pilot's diagnosis had said: the new contents are not shared; choose a slice where contexts recur. The decimal slice supplied what was asked. Of its 213 new contents, 7 occur in more than one minimal declared set, against the pilot's one — and all seven are contexts, one content of a two-content remainder. Earned entailments: 21 before, 21 after.

The earned entailment that is false

Of the register's earned pairs, all but the four closure pairs of the final section come from its older, denser corpora, and one of them deserves a picture of its own. incompatibility_earned_entails(even, composite, 1) is in the checked artifact today, and it is materially false: 2 is even and prime. Figure 4 places it beside dog and mammal.

Until today the mirror ran both ways. The fixture itself declared {mammal, feline}, materially false, so the control earned its true conclusion partly from a false set while the seeds earn a false conclusion from three true ones, and the relation registered "passed" and "earned" alike. The corrected fixture declares three true sets, and the two columns of Figure 4 are still the same picture: everything the register computes survives the relabeling, and material truth does not. The difference between its control and its embarrassment lies outside anything it can consult.

The module comment over the relation (formal/incompatibility/brandomian_incompatibility.pl) predicts this in its own words: sparse data yields too-strong entailments, as a property of the data. Since today the register also marks the pair from inside, incompatibility_earned_entailment_support(even, composite, sparse_witness) — computed, kept, and flagged as resting on a single declared set. Marking is as far as inside goes: thinness is a fact about declared structure, falsity is not. And the moral still runs against the repair nearest to hand. Thin sharing buys false entailments before it buys true ones, so a push that nudged the 90 triples toward earning by light sharing would mint pairs of this kind first. The corpus's zero is the honest value of data one incompatibility fact deep.

What the corpus yields instead

A great deal of careful coding aimed at a count that could not move. Those two facts together are the finding. The expansions were the experiment that located the block, and the location is now proven where it was only suspected. There is a genuine tension underneath: the coding spec that maximizes emergent sets keeps every set minimal at three members, sharing nothing the mathematics does not force, and that is precisely the spec that ships a private pair in every set. Emergence and earned entailment cannot be climbed by the same coding act in this shape. The boundary deserves its name in this project's vocabulary, a vanishing point: the place where the formalization honestly stops. What stops it here is the depth of the declared data, one incompatibility fact per rule, rather than the relation or the corpus's subject matter.

What the register holds natively from the same work: 94 emergent minimal incompatible sets, 90 of them coded error-rule triples, each carrying the name of the community whose sanction warrants it (error_rule_warrant/2). And the shared contexts, inert for entailment, land as a different result: hubs. "The divisor is not a whole number" defeats four distinct student rules; "the expansion does not terminate" defeats three. An input family that confronts four rules at once is a cross-rule finding the entailment order cannot state, and for teaching it marks the high-leverage counterexample class.

The nesting turned out to be the honest route, and it has now been taken. Eleven closure triples are declared (formal/incompatibility/incompatibility_sets_a_fortiori_context_closure.pl), generated from four reviewed nestings in formal/incompatibility/a_fortiori_context_nestings.json, each row carrying a recorded warrant and basis. A battery (scripts/checks/a_fortiori_context_closure_automaton_battery.pl) provides check evidence that the loaded comparison automata decide none of the shipped nestings, so no warrant is sold as an automaton certificate; and one name-plausible nesting was refused at review, because "the divisor is not ten" and "the divisor is not a whole number" nest in neither direction. A rule defeated on a class is defeated on its subclasses, so each broad-context rule is re-declared as defeated on the nested narrower contexts — and earned entailment moved for the first time, 21 to 25. The narrow divisor context earns the broad one with 4 witnesses; the expansion contexts earn as a chain, repeating nines inside repeating periodically inside non-terminating. Every one of the four is materially true, every one arrived by a new shape of declared set, and the count of coded triples did not change.

What the closure could not do is also now recorded. The relation cannot distinguish its control from its embarrassment (Figure 4); the level shift that made the boundary productive — articulating the presupposed relations among contexts in order to test them — was performed outside the register and re-entered as data. Limits

The impossibility argument quantifies over declared sets only, like the relation itself. New set shapes void it by design; the closure declared today is that observation acted on, and the earned count moved exactly then. Material judgments on this page — the falsity of even-entails-composite, the soundness of the corrected fixture, the four context nestings — are checkable human judgments; the nestings carry recorded warrants and bases in their source file, and the register asserts none of the material claims. Earned counts recomputed today: 25 in the current register (21 plus the four closure pairs), 21 in the pre-decimal register from git history; the pre-pilot 21 is carried by the session records and was not independently recomputed here.
