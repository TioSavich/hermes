# Task 125 — the shared action alphabet

Model: `claude-opus-5[1m]` (Opus 5, 1M context).
Date: 2026-07-24.
Files written: `knowledge/strategies/action_vocabulary_map.pl`,

## What the wall was

Tasks 116 and 119 ended at a measurement, not an opinion: 638 distinct action
labels across 172 automaton signatures, 547 of them used by exactly one
signature. Among the 69 execution-witnessed automata, no two share a minimized
structure and none maps into another by a rooted action-preserving map.
Conservative string clustering found one candidate join in the whole corpus, and
it was between two state names, not two actions.

That is not a fact about mathematics. It is a fact about how the tables were
written: one machine at a time, each author naming the step in the vocabulary of
that machine's topic. `iterations`, `iterate_unit_fraction`,
`iterate_interval_from_zero`, `place_equal_unit_squares`, and `iterate_rows` are
five names for repeating a unit. Nothing short of reading each one in its machine
tells you that.

## What was authored

**An alphabet of 80 canonical actions** — 40 carrying a citation to the
literature or to `state_vocabulary.pl`'s attributed labels, 40 named in-house
with the reason recorded in a `coined/1` term. 644 local labels (the census's 638
declared ones plus 6 that appear only on execution-observed edges) reduce to 80.

**785 `action_maps/7` rows**, one per `(family, signature, label)` triple the
transition tables actually contain: 367 on the 69 witnessed machines, 418 on the
102 static-only ones. Confidence: 722 high, 61 medium, 2 low.

**2 `action_unmapped/4` rows** — `measurement/drop_unit_from_measured_quantity_change`
and `measurement/unit_preserving_measured_quantity_change` both use
`perform_grounded_quantity_change`, which names a change in a measured quantity
without naming its direction. The alphabet's grounded operations are directional
(`combine_quantities`, `remove_quantity`), so any assignment would assert a
direction the table declines to assert. The remainder is the finding: it names a
place where the alphabet is missing something rather than a place where the
tables are.

Every row's evidence names the states the edge connects, how many of the
machine's distinct edges the row covers, whether any of those edges is
execution-witnessed, and what the step does there. No row rests on the local
label resembling the canonical name; the check enforces the first half of that
mechanically (evidence must name its own signature and a state transition) and
the second half is on the record for the owner to disagree with row by row.

### The two rules that decided the hard cases

Both are stated in the module docstring so that a reader can reject them.

**Position.** A terminal edge into the accepting state whose label names what the
strategy kept or lost maps to `record_conservation` or `record_loss`. The same
word at an intermediate edge maps to what it does *there*. This is why
`preserve_data_set`, which is the first edge of nine statistics machines, is
`register_givens`, while `preserve_whole_area`, which is the last edge of
`area_preserving_polygon_decomposition`, is `record_conservation`; and why
`preserve_equal_shares`, sitting mid-machine in `fair_share_equal_groups`, is
`verify_invariant` — it certifies the shares before the naming step, it does not
close the machine.

**Slot.** Where a deformation and its viable partner differ at one edge, the
deformation's label is read against the slot the partner fills. Three examples
carried real weight:

- `numerator_denominator_gap` in `gap_thinking_fraction_comparison` stands where
  `benchmark_fraction_comparison` has `judgment`. It is therefore
  `substitute_available_relation`, not a reading of an attribute: the additive
  numerator-denominator distance stands in for a judgment against the benchmark.
  That is Pearn & Stephens's gap thinking, which the projected structure now
  carries as one edge.
- `overcount` in `number_line_count_marks_not_intervals` stands where
  `number_line_fraction_comparison` has `iterations`. Same reading: the mark
  count stands in for iterating the unit interval (Bright, Behr, Post &
  Wachsmuth 1988).
- `shaded_pieces_removed_from_partition_structure` stands where
  `area_model_fraction_comparison` has `shaded_parts`. The viable one is
  `disembed_part` — the part comes out and stays inside the whole (Hackenberg
  2013; Steffe & Olive 2010). The deformation is
  `treat_relevant_as_irrelevant`: the pieces are taken *out of* the partition
  structure, so they no longer answer to the partition that made them. Removal
  is not disembedding, and the alphabet holds them apart.

### One label, two doings

`establish_base` names the grouping base of a positional numeral in
`counting/recursive_place_value_inscription` and the repeated factor of a power
in `algebraic/exponent_as_repeated_factor`. Those are different doings wearing
one string, so the two occurrences carry different canonical actions
(`select_unit_scale` and `assign_roles`). It is the only such divergence in the
corpus. The census's other 19 "shared-role" labels turn out to be the same doing
at a different position in the machine — `traverse_side` at the start state and
at an intermediate state, `plot_endpoint` twice in a row — and those keep one
canonical action each.

### Where a viable strategy sets something aside

Three labels look like deformations and are not. `ignore_nondefining_orientation`
in `shape_classification_by_defining_attributes`, `ignore_arrangement_extent` in
`compare_solid_volume_by_cube_count`, and `ignore_decimal_marks_momentarily` in
`decimal_multiplication_rule` all set aside a property the conclusion genuinely
does not depend on. They map to `set_aside_irrelevant_attribute`, which exists
precisely so that `treat_relevant_as_irrelevant` can stay honest. Mapping all
`ignore_*` labels together would have manufactured a deformation out of correct
mathematical judgment.

### Risk gating

`knowledge/crosswalk/vocabulary_licenses.pl` marks 36 of its 79 entries HIGH:
same word for different things, or different words for the same thing, in ways
its author found likely to mislead students. No HIGH entry licensed a merge here.
Where a canonical action's citation names a HIGH entry, it names it as the source
of a term and records the disambiguation obligation; the check fails the file if
a HIGH citation omits that. The division case is enforced structurally rather
than by prose: vl005 (sharing) and vl006 (measurement) are both HIGH *because*
students confuse them, so `share_into_known_groups` and `measure_out_group_size`
are two canonical actions, and the check fails if any label maps to both.

The MEDIUM and LOW entries did carry weight. vl004 (Compare, MEDIUM) is the one
comparison term the crosswalk marks substitutable across all four content
frameworks, and it licenses `compare_magnitudes` collecting fourteen local
labels. vl027 (equivalent fractions, LOW) licenses `re_express_equivalently`.

`fraction/add_numerator_denominator_comparison` carries two edges out of
`q_add_numerator_denominator` whose labels (`first`, `second`) both map to

is demonstrably too coarse for the machine it is applied to, and the analyzer
says so in its own output rather than in a footnote.

### The coincidence count stays at zero, and that is the honest result

The compression is real and the coincidence is not. No two of the 69 have the
same projected structure. The reason is checkable: reduce each machine to the
word its minimized structure spells (68 of the 69 are a single linear chain) and
no two words are equal. So the map compresses the vocabulary hard — 276 actions
to 74 — without making any two strategies structurally the same. The
inviolable constraint was never tested, because nothing came close enough to test
it.

The homomorphism count stays at zero for a reason that belongs to the analyzer
rather than to the vocabulary: `homomorphism/2` requires accepting-status
agreement along the map, so a shorter machine can never map into a longer one's
prefix even when its whole action word is that prefix. Under projection, zero
words are a strict prefix of another anyway, so the two zeros do not
distinguish the cases here. If the owner wants prefix embeddings surfaced, that
is a change to the analyzer's homomorphism criterion, not to this map.

### What the three new near-coincidences say

These are the pairs that came within one canonical action of coinciding, which
they did not do at exact-label grain. Each is a finding with both signatures
named, and none is a merge.

**`addition/count_on_from_larger` and `multiplication/repeat_equal_groups`.**

    assign_roles -> assign_roles -> count_on_from        -> name_result
    assign_roles -> assign_roles -> replicate_equal_groups -> name_result

Both bind two quantities to their roles, run an iteration, and name the total.
They differ at exactly one step, and the difference is the unit the iteration
repeats: a successor tick against a composite group. That is the units-coordination
step of the Steffe/Olive/Hackenberg line, arriving as the single edge two
otherwise identical machines disagree on. I think this one is real and worth
saying out loud in a methods class: counting on and repeated equal groups are the
same strategy shape at two levels of unit.

**`division/name_group_count_as_share_size` and `multiplication/repeat_group_size_by_itself`.**

    assign_roles -> conflate_roles -> misname_result         -> record_loss
    assign_roles -> conflate_roles -> replicate_equal_groups -> record_loss

Two deformations in two different operations with the same shape: bind the roles,
collapse two of them, then either name the wrong quantity or iterate the wrong
one, and lose the relation. That the shape recurs across operations is the kind of
thing the exact-label corpus could not reach. I am less confident this one is
deep. Both machines are four edges long, and their first edge is `assign_roles`,
the alphabet's third-broadest action at 41 rows drawn from 32 distinct labels. A
shape this short whose opening comes from an action that wide can coincide by
coarseness alone. `conflate_roles` in the second position is narrow by
comparison (7 rows, 7 labels), so the agreement there carries more weight than
the agreement at the first edge.

**`fraction/number_line_count_marks_not_intervals` and
`fraction/number_line_fraction_comparison`.**

    initiate -> unitize_referent -> partition_into_equal_parts
      -> substitute_available_relation -> locate_position
      -> align_to_common_unit -> compare_magnitudes -> emit_result
    initiate -> unitize_referent -> partition_into_equal_parts
      -> iterate_unit                -> locate_position
      -> align_to_common_unit -> compare_magnitudes -> emit_result

Eight edges, agreeing on seven. This is the designed viable/deformation pair, and
the projection puts the whole difference at one edge: where the viable machine
iterates the unit interval, the deformation substitutes the mark count. That is
what the pair was built to say, and it is the first time the tables carry it
structurally rather than by their file layout. This one I trust.

The two pre-existing near-coincidences (`decimal_add_unaligned_numerals` against
`decimal_subtract_unaligned_numerals`, and the aligned-units pair) differ at
`combine_quantities` against `remove_quantity` — which is exactly where they must
differ, since vl001 and vl002 are both HIGH risk and the crosswalk records that
students confuse joining with separating. The map holding them one action apart,
and not zero, is the risk gating doing its job.

### The shared idioms

Under projection, the bounded subtraces two machines share stop being
topic-local. The ones recurring across the most pairs:

| pairs | subtrace |
|---:|---|
| 15 | `compare_magnitudes -> emit_result` |
| 10 | `align_to_common_unit -> compare_magnitudes` |
| 10 | `misname_result -> record_loss` |
| 10 | `initiate -> unitize_referent` |
| 6 | `align_to_common_unit -> compare_magnitudes -> emit_result` |
| 6 | `assign_roles -> assign_roles` |
| 6 | `unitize_referent -> partition_into_equal_parts` |
| 6 | `substitute_available_relation -> substitute_available_relation` |

At exact-label grain the same table's top entry was `compare -> emit` across 6
pairs, and everything below it was the decimal family's `align_decimal_units`
chain talking to itself. The projected table is the first cross-family evidence
in this corpus that the machines share idioms at all: unitize then partition,
align then compare, misname then record the loss.

`substitute_available_relation -> substitute_available_relation` across 6 pairs is
the one I would flag as suspect. That action collects 27 rows spanning
whole-number dominance, counting marks for intervals, adding where the problem
subtracts, reading equals as an instruction, and privileging a prototype
orientation. Two consecutive substitutions in two machines being "the same
idiom" is very likely an artifact of that breadth rather than a shared doing.
Splitting `substitute_available_relation` along a principled seam — perhaps by
what kind of thing is substituted, a quantity for a quantity against a reading
for a reading — is the obvious next refinement, and I did not attempt it because
the seam is not obvious to me from the tables alone.

## Honest limits

- **The alphabet is mine, not the field's.** Half its names are coined. Every
  coined name carries its reason, and 40 of 80 carry a citation, but a reader
  from the Steffe line and a reader from Van de Walle would each carve some of
  these differently. The rows are `status(review_pending)` because they are.
- **Confidence is self-assessed.** 722 high, 61 medium, 2 low. The two low rows
  (`change_decimal_unit_name`, `change_unit_label`) name the clearest gap: both
  put a *name* where a relation was required, and the alphabet has no canonical
  action for relabelling, so they land on `substitute_available_relation` as the
  closest of a poor fit. With the unmapped `perform_grounded_quantity_change`,
  that is three concrete candidates for extending the alphabet.
- **`register_givens`, `read_operand_attribute`, and `assign_roles` carry 144 of
  the 785 rows between them.** The opening moves of a strategy are where the
  alphabet is coarsest, and where a coincidence is least informative. Any future
  finding that turns on those three actions alone should be treated as an
  artifact until something else corroborates it.
- **418 of the 785 rows are on machines with no execution-observed edges.** They
  are authored from the static extraction and are unexercised by the analyzer,
  which only reads witnessed automata. They will matter when more machines get
  witnessed; until then they are untested by anything but the check.
- **The projection is not applied anywhere the app can reach.** The map is
  quarantined: no recognizer, reader, or automaton consults it, and the analyzer
  reads it only when asked. Nothing in the running application changed.

## For the owner

Three things need a decision, and none of them blocks anything:

1. **The three unfilled slots** — a direction-neutral quantity change, a
   relabelling action, and possibly a split of
   `substitute_available_relation`. Extending the alphabet is cheap; deciding
   the seams is not, and they are vocabulary decisions about the framework
   rather than about the code.
2. **`fraction/add_numerator_denominator_comparison`'s `first`/`second` edges.**
   The map cannot project them without making the machine nondeterministic. Either
   the alphabet gains a way to say "the first pair, then the second," or the
   table's two edges get labels that already say it, or the machine stays at
   exact-label grain permanently. The analyzer currently takes the third option
   and reports it.
3. **Whether `homomorphism/2` should accept prefix embeddings.** Right now
   accepting-status agreement forbids them. It changes what "one strategy is
   contained in another" means, so it is a formal-core question, not a tuning
   knob.
