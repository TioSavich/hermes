# The saying and doing of grade 8, read against the kernel–gate formula

Date: 2026-08-05. Author: Claude (Fable 5), as a read-only survey; no repo file
changed but this one. Revised 2026-08-06 for readers arriving from outside the
project. Everything quoted carries its path. Everything reconstructed is marked
as such.

Two questions drive this report. First: the project carries a one-line
compression of its own architecture — *strategy = gate ▷ shell[kernels]; error
= mutation or unlicensed transfer; mathematics = the institutionalized repair
of a refusal* — and the compression arrived in conversation rather than in any
file, so its clauses needed to be anchored to what the repository actually
holds. Second: the corpus of strategy automata stops at grade 5, and grade 8 —
where the letter x enters the curriculum — is unenacted territory. Does the
formula extend there? The conjecture under test, the manuscript author's: the
field of possibility a variable ranges over is opened by recursive partitioning
— the fractal of fractions — so the grade-8 letter presupposes exactly the
closure the fraction rung institutionalizes.

## 1. The formula, clause by clause

The one-line compression appears in no repository file; the "▷" composition
glyph occurs nowhere in the tree. The sentence is a reconstruction. Each of its
clauses, though, has an exact in-repo referent, quoted below verbatim. The
one-liner is reconstruction; its parts are recovery.

### 1.1 strategy = gate ▷ shell[kernels]

The formula's canonical form lives at
`knowledge/strategies/abstraction/kernel_gate_pilot.pl:4-6`:

> An executable pilot of the formulation
>
>     strategy = gate(genre, representation) |> shell [ kernel ]
>
> on three cross-domain probes: complete_to_unit, iterate_to_target,
> and partition/regroup. The kernel carries control flow and actually
> computes. The gate supplies sorts, admissibility guards, the named
> boundary, and result interpretation. Correct and incorrect doings are
> instances or single local mutations of the same kernel run.

### 1.2 error = mutation or unlicensed transfer

The pilot names two genres of error (`kernel_gate_pilot.pl:35-53`): "KERNEL
MUTATIONS act inside a gate: drop a part, pick the wrong output, miscount by
one, evade a refusal by swapping operands. ... GATE MUTATIONS break the sort
discipline itself: conflating the count of units with the size of the unit
(adding across denominators), or using a relational token as a sequencing move
(5-3=2=3-5; 'pokey dog is')." The "unlicensed transfer" half is sharpened in
`refusal_genesis_sketch.pl:9-24`: "the diagnosis of a gate mutation is not 'the
sort was collapsed' but 'WHICH gate licenses this move, and what did the
transfer forget.' ... In scorekeeping terms: the student claims an entitlement
by inheritance from a practice where it was earned, and the inheritance is what
fails."

### 1.3 mathematics = the institutionalized repair of a refusal

`refusal_genesis_sketch.pl:26-46`, whose closing sentence is the clause's

> A misconception and a number system are the same move — a license
> transported past a refusal — under different scorekeeping: the
> student's transfer goes unvindicated; the institution's is made
> coherent and becomes a gate.

The ladder itself is data (`refusal_genesis_sketch.pl:127-132`):

genesis(whole_number(_), refused(non_integer_share), unit_fraction(_)).

"Determinate negation is the generator at every rung" is the fractal report's

The same report holds the measured claim behind "shell[kernels]": the

closure, and the honest limit that 28% do not fit and were not diagnosed. The

"kernel" is enacted in the live corpus, with `fraction/splitting` the strongest

The conversation that generated this line of work is preserved at

### 1.4 The denominator's two names

An intuition worth taking apart preceded this survey: that a denominator works
like a numeral base — "1/7 is kinda like 1 in base 7." The repository answers
with two names at two loci, and they divide the labor that "base" packs into
one word.

**Inside one fraction, the denominator is the gate's boundary, read as a
partitioned whole.** `kernel_gate_pilot.pl:134-140`:

```prolog
gate(unit_fraction(D),
     props(sort(count(unit_fraction(D))),
           boundary(D), boundary_kind(partitioned_whole(D)),
           operative_radix(10), lower_limit(0), subtraction(partial))).
```

The base intuition is in the code, and it is deliberately held as a licensed
identification rather than an equation. `kernel_gate_pilot.pl:114-118`: "The
fraction gate carries BOTH an operative radix and a unit boundary, and they are
independent parameters — sevenths written in base-ten numerals have boundary 7
and radix 10." And :323-328: "bridge(From, To, psi(Scale)): the licensed
identification. psi carries a count of ones under radix cycle B to a count of
unit fractions 1/B under the partitioned whole. It is a fact a translation must
cite — never applied silently, because the radix cycle and the partitioned
whole are different things that happen to be isomorphic as bounded complement
structures." The row: `bridge(whole_number(B), unit_fraction(B),
psi(scale(B)))`. So the honest form of the intuition: 1/7 and 1-in-base-7 are
isomorphic as bounded complement structures, and the isomorphism is a citable
license, never an identity.

**Between two fractions, the denominator work is co-measurement.** The state
`q_measure_with_co_unit` runs through six fraction automata
(`knowledge/strategies/transition_tables/fraction.pl`), and its blessed label
is `knowledge/strategies/math/state_vocabulary.pl:147`:

```prolog
state_label(q_measure_with_co_unit, constructivist, "co-measurement unit",
            "Shin & Lee 2018; Nabors 2003").
```

`refusal_genesis_sketch.pl` then makes co_measure the single kernel the
fraction rung costs: "try to derive the complex practice by composing existing
kernels under gates; a new kernel is admitted only when the derivation fails,
and each admission is the finding. Here: non-common-denominator addition costs
exactly ONE new kernel (co_measure — find the common refinement of two units)."
Fraction measurement division then costs zero further kernels: "a shell that
loops the iterate kernel downward and uses the GATE'S OWN REFUSAL as its
stopping condition." And co_measure is the kernel whose refusal at
(side, diagonal) is the top of the ladder:
`run_co_measure(uf(_), len(irrational(N)), refused(incommensurable(N)))`.

Nesting, the fractal-of-fractions move itself, is one comment and one predicate
(`kernel_gate_pilot.pl:245`): "Nesting: partition the part. 1/7 of 1/7 is 1/49
of the referent."

So: **boundary of a partitioned whole** within one fraction; **co-measurement
unit** between two. "Base" survives only as the licensed bridge psi.

## 2. The saying and doing of grade 8

Source: the 134 Illustrative Mathematics grade-8 teacher lesson guides, held as
Docling-extracted markdown in this machine's local runtime store (the guides
are licensed curriculum and do not ship in the tree). The nine units hold 17,
13, 15, 16, 22, 11, 16, 18, and 6 lessons; 33 lessons were read across all
nine units. Docling strips much inline math, so some task statements lost their
expressions; where a quote has a gap, the gap is Docling's. Unit titles are
inferred from lesson headings. In the repository's curriculum layer, all 134
grade-8 rows are `scope_sequence_only_lesson` (`curriculum/im/grade_8.pl`): no
lesson is tied to any automaton. Grade 8 is unenacted territory.

The commission asked for grade 8's *saying* and *doing* separately, and the
split turns out to be the curriculum's own format: the guides' Goals sections
carry the saying explicitly ("describe orally and in writing," "justify,"
"generalize") and the tasks carry the doing.

## 3. The conjecture, worked rung by rung

### 3.1 The fraction rung as authored

Slot by slot: gate = `unit_fraction(D)`, sort a count of unit fractions,
boundary D read as `partitioned_whole(D)`, subtraction partial, lower limit 0.
Kernels = partition_regroup (the one-and-many coordination, self-composing:
`nested_partition`), complete_to_unit, iterate_to_target, and the rung's one
admitted kernel, co_measure. Shells = `splitting` (receives outcomes from a
partition kernel and an iterate kernel), the co_denominator automata
dispatching to the CGI kernel, and `measure_out`, which loops the iterate
kernel with the gate's own refusal as its stopping condition. Errors = kernel
mutations (off_by_one, drop_part, select_output, evade_refusal) against one
gate mutation, conflate_count_with_unit_size, whose product is the mediant,
held per-input as contextually_correct or incorrect (the viability context).
Genesis = the ladder's middle rung: the whole-number gate refuses the
non-integer share; the unit-fraction gate licenses it.

### 3.2 What grade 8's variable-bearing doings ask of each slot

**Gate.** For U4's unknown, the gate is already sayable from the guides: sort =
quantity-denoting expressions; ledger = everything flanking "=" denotes one
quantity, and every move conserves the solution set; admissible operations =
the named moves of 4-1; result interpretation = a solution set that may be
empty or total (4-15 asks students to categorize exactly this). The guides
teach the gate AS a saying: "justify that each step maintains equality."

**Kernels.** Substitute-and-evaluate exists twice over in the algebraic family
(`equation_truth_by_substitution`, `programming_expression_evaluation`, the
latter already delegating into grounded arithmetic through `kernel_trace`, so
the delegation register already crosses into algebra). Apply-balance-move
exists as `balance_preserving_linear_solution`'s spine. Apply-rule-to-input is
the function kernel; the 5-1 game is a shell searching kernel-space.

**Errors.** The rung's central gate mutation is already doubly authored.
`refusal_genesis_sketch.pl:111-113`:

```prolog
antecedent(equals_as_arrow,
           license(procedure_genre, sequencing(then)),
           forgets(equality_is_symmetric_and_flanks_one_quantity)).
```

and enacted as the two deformation automata `one_sided_equation_operation`
(`read_equals_as_instruction`) and `operational_equals_left_value`
(`treat_equals_as_answer_signal`) in `transition_tables/algebraic.pl`. On the
error slot, the formula extends to grade 8 without strain: the misconception
is a license transported from the procedure genre into assertion, and the
guides' U4 sayings are precisely the counter-training.

**Where the recursion needs a new move.** The gate ladder as authored extends
object sorts: counts, then signed counts, then unit-fraction counts, then
segment-numbers. Each genesis row repairs the refusal of an operation on
objects. The variable is a different negative. What "x stands for any number"
repairs is a refusal of the practice of forward computation itself: 3 + _ = 7
cannot be computed forward, and the unknown institutionalizes the entitlement
to assert a relation before any value is in hand. That negation lands on the
practice slot, where the ladder's negations land on the object slot. If
determinate negation is the generator at every rung, negation recurring at a
new layer is the self-similarity, per the fractal report's polarity
correction. But the two rungs are the same move only at the level of the
generator; the slot negated differs, and saying so precisely is the honest
form of the extension.

### 3.3 Steelman of the conjecture

The strongest reading, stated so it can lose. The unit fraction
institutionalizes "one part of an arbitrary partition"; partition composes with
itself (`nested_partition`: 1/7 of 1/7 is 1/49 of the referent); co_measure
computes a common refinement for every pair of unit sizes, so the practice of
recursive partitioning carries the density of the rationals inside itself. For
a letter to denote in equations like 2x = 3, the value-field must be closed
under the inverse of iteration, which is partition; so the entitlement to
write the equation and treat x as denoting presupposes exactly the partitional
closure the fraction rung institutionalizes. Grade 8 then exhibits the limit
of that presupposition in its own sequence: x² = 2 is refused by every
partition (co_measure's authored refusal: `refused(incommensurable(sqrt2))`),
and the curriculum repairs by inscription: "If the solution is irrational,
write the solution using square root or cube root notation" (8-15). That is
the formula's third clause performed in a lesson: the refusal is named, and
the name is institutionalized. The gate ladder's top rung
(`genesis(measuring_stick, refused(incommensurable), real_line)`) is Unit 8's
literal content, from the tilted squares of 8-1 to the root notation of 8-15.
On this reading the conjecture holds: the field of possibility x ranges over
is opened by recursive partitioning and completed by the institutionalized
repair of partition's one great refusal, and grade 8 is where the curriculum
performs the top of the ladder.

### 3.4 Rebuttal, at full strength

Four counts, each anchored in the census.

1. **Order.** Grade 8 grants the variable in U3, U4, and U5, and partition
   vocabulary is absent from all three. The letter enters naming a context
   quantity ("$2.50 is subtracted ... after r rides"), and its range is
   context-typed and sometimes discrete (Noah's rides are whole numbers). "For
   x to truly stand for anything" describes the algebraist's x; the grade-8 x
   does not require it, and the guides never invoke it.
2. **Covariation.** U5 grounds the letter in dependency, and asks it as a
   saying: "Name the two quantities in this situation that are in a functional
   relationship. Which did you choose to be the independent variable?" (5-8).
   The research community behind this reading (Confrey and Smith on
   covariation; Thompson on quantitative reasoning) holds that function-letters
   arise from coordinating two changing quantities, and the census sides with
   them for U3 through U6. Partition appears nowhere in the covariational
   doings.
3. **History.** The letter arises from generalized arithmetic and rhetoric
   about unknowns (al-jabr; Viète's species), and the density of its domain is
   a 19th-century repair (Dedekind). The desire-genesis of the variable, the
   negative that wants a value, is a philosophical claim; curriculum data can
   exhibit an order of institution and nothing deeper, and the order it
   exhibits puts covariation first and the continuum last.
4. **The formula's own slots.** Nothing in gate ▷ shell[kernels] requires the
   gate universe to be ordered by genesis. The variable can be read as a gate
   over gates: quantifying over assignments is quantifying over runs, and the
   pilot's `translate_run/3` changes a gate slot inside a run, where the
   variable makes the gate slot itself the object. That is second-order
   recursion, and the ladder as authored is first-order. The steelman and the
   rebuttal agree on this much: recursive partitioning opens the value-field;
   it does not by itself produce the practice of letting a letter denote an
   arbitrary member. Two negations, and they should not be collapsed.

One reconciliation is available and worth naming precisely. In the census's
own order, students use the variable (U3–U5) before its domain is complete
(U8). The letter is granted while the field is still open; the field's
incompleteness is encountered at x² = 2 and repaired by inscription. If the
desire-reading has an empirical shadow, it is this: the unknown runs ahead of
what any partition can supply, and the curriculum lets it. But curriculum
order is an institutional artifact, and reading conceptual genesis off it
would be an overclaim.

## 4. What could be built

The algebraic family holds 14 automata (`transition_tables/algebraic.pl`):
nine productive (balance_preserving_linear_solution,
contextual_linear_equation_construction, distributive_expression_rewrite,
equation_truth_by_substitution, exponent_as_repeated_factor,
exponential_equivalence_by_expansion, linear_pattern_contextual_rule,
programming_expression_evaluation, symbolic_expression_construction) and five
deformation partners (drop_distributed_term, exponent_as_multiplier,
guess_and_check_rule, one_sided_equation_operation,
operational_equals_left_value). They were authored from research extracts
(Gray 1999; Lannin 2005), never from these guides. What grade 8 already gets
from them: equation truth, balance solving, expression evaluation, contextual
construction, exponent expansion, pattern rules. What it does not get: the
move-level equivalence discipline of U4, slope, functions as input-output
coordination, and everything in U8.

Candidate automata if the partitional reading is right — proposals, not code
that exists — each named with its slots:

1. **algebraic/equivalent_move_ladder** (U4-1, 4-5). Gate: equation_relation
   (ledger: denotation conserved across =, solution set conserved by moves).
   Kernel: apply_inverse_operation_to_both_sides. Shell: select the move that
   advances isolation. Deformations: one_sided_equation_operation (attested,
   exists); candidates divide_by_expression_losing_solution and
   move_without_inverse. The guides ask students to NAME each move ("Next to
   each arrow, write what the move is"), so the automaton's action alphabet is
   the lesson's own saying, a rare direct alignment.
2. **algebraic/slope_co_measurement** (U2-10, U3-5). Gate: ratio_invariance
   under dilation (the license that all slope triangles are similar, which
   2-10 makes students justify). Kernel: co_measure lifted to two dimensions,
   the rise counted with the run as its unit. Deformations:
   additive_gap_for_slope (subtracting where the practice divides) and
   sign_blind_slope (U3-9). This is the strongest partitional bridge into
   grade 8: it carries the co-measurement-unit vocabulary directly, with the
   run as the co-unit.
3. **algebraic/function_rule_coordination** (U5-1, 5-3, 5-8). Gate:
   input_output_dependency (roles: independent/dependent; ledger: one input
   never two outputs). Kernel: apply_rule (programming_expression_evaluation
   is this kernel already). Shell: guess_rule_from_pairs, the 5-1 game as a
   search shell. Deformations: guess_and_check_rule (attested, exists);
   candidates swap_dependency_roles and rule_from_one_pair.
4. **algebraic/nested_interval_root** (U8-6, 8-12, 8-15). Gate: real_line,
   whose genesis row exists. Kernels: iterate_to_target (squeeze between
   consecutive wholes) composed with partition_regroup (refine the interval).
   Shell: a precision budget whose exhaustion inscribes the root symbol, the
   same control shape as measure_out, where the refusal is the stopping
   condition. Deformations: truncate_as_exact (1.41 asserted as the value of
   √2) and root_as_halving. This automaton would run the conjecture as code:
   recursive partitioning enacted on the number line until the gate refuses,
   with the inscription of the root as the institutionalized repair.
5. **algebraic/rule_generalization_from_cases** (U7-4). Gate: pattern-to-rule
   (ledger: the rule must survive the next case). Kernel:
   exponent_as_repeated_factor (exists). Shell: the induction step over a
   table. Deformation: rule_without_ground, already an operator in the
   regularization conversation's closed set.

**What would decide between the partitional and covariational readings.**
Three levers, cheapest first. (a) Extend the census to all 134 Goals sections
and count vocabulary: partition-words (part, unit, divide into, between
consecutive) against covariation-words (rate, change, depends, input, output)
per unit; the 33-lesson sample predicts covariation dominates U3–U6 with
partition concentrated in U8, and that prediction is falsifiable by a
grep-and-classify pass. (b) Author candidate automata 2 and 3 and test which
family's deformations student work on the same tasks attests; the repository
currently holds no grade-8 student corpus, so this lever waits on data.
(c) Prove or refute the regularization conversation's candidate theorem that
nested unit composition exceeds finite-state elaboration; if it holds, the
partitional reading gains its formal spine (the stack IS the recursion of
partition), and if it fails, the fractal claim for fractions loses its
strongest formal support.

## 5. Honest limits

- **Reconstruction, marked.** The one-line formula with "▷" is a compression
  from conversation, in no repository file. Its clauses are anchored to
  `kernel_gate_pilot.pl`, `refusal_genesis_sketch.pl`, the fractal report, and
  the regularization conversation, all quoted above; the composition into one
  sentence is not recoverable from the tree and is treated here as the
  manuscript author's.
- **The abstraction wing is quarantined.** Nothing imports
  `kernel_gate_pilot.pl` or `refusal_genesis_sketch.pl`; their rows are
  authored and vetoable; the ladder has three rungs and the middle one is
  stated, never run. Building on them means promoting them, which is a scope
  decision the survey does not make.
- **The census is a sample.** 33 of 134 lessons, all nine units. Docling
  strips inline math, so several quoted tasks lost their expressions
  (bracketed letters above are restorations from context). Image-description
  text in the guides is a vision model's paraphrase and was not treated as
  ground truth. Unit titles are inferred from lesson headings.
- **Grade 8 is unenacted.** All 134 curriculum rows are scope_sequence_only,
  and no live probe ties the algebraic automata to these lessons; they were
  authored from research extracts, not from this curriculum.
- **What the data cannot say.** The desire-genesis of the variable, and any
  claim that the continuum must be synthesized before a letter can denote,
  are philosophical claims. The census can report vocabulary, order, and
  doings; it found covariation first, partition late and localized, and one
  lesson (8-15) that performs the institutionalized inscription of a refusal
  in so many words. Whether that lesson is the top of the ladder or a
  notational convenience is exactly the question candidate automaton 4 would
  make executable, and nothing short of building it settles the matter.
