Run: `generate_negative_receipts.py --grades K,1,2,3,4,5 --limit 80` against the
ledger at `cd6af53`+receipts, the first run at the inverted pool (76 K-5 lessons

Review: two independent readers over disjoint halves (0-24, 25-49), the
failure-mode taxonomy of `2026-07-27` in the prompt, automaton semantics read
for every intended/alternative pair. **37 keep / 13 reject.** The 37 merged into
`scripts/curriculum/lesson_negative_receipts.json` (109 -> 146 rows);
`diagnostic_ready` 237 -> 274. The validator's bite was confirmed before the
clean build: one corrupted fragment character refused the whole build, naming
the drift and its line.

Two systematic findings beyond the decisions:

- Whole-number division automata (`positive_int` divisor guards) appear as
  negative candidates on grade-5 fraction-division lessons, guaranteeing
  cannot-run rejects. A candidate-contract filter is queued.
- The drafting prompt glosses `dropped_ones_chunk` as "ignoring the composed
  ten"; the automaton drops the second addend's ones chunk. Three receipts
  survived on their quantities despite the gloss; the prompt needs the fix
  next pass.
