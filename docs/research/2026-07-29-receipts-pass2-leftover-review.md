# Receipts pass-2 leftovers — review record (2026-07-29)

Ten pass-2 receipts (grade 6-7, digest-sourced) were drafted 2026-07-27 and
never reviewed; three further pass-2 rows fell out of criterion because a
different-alternative receipt for their lesson already merged. All quoted
fragments confirmed verbatim in the vision digest. **1 keep / 9 reject**
(10%, below the 39% digest base rate - this pool was picked over twice).

Two systematic findings, both actionable upstream of review:

- `vision_lesson_boundary` is two texts wearing one predicate: transcribed
  curriculum AND a second-pass revision log. Four rejects quoted the log;
  two quoted the exact sentence stating the demand has NO automaton. The
  drafter prompt gains a disqualifying-vocabulary rule (first pass,
  reclassif, re-tagged, mis-tagged, DELETED, CORRECTION, no automaton,
  kind NONE, opening 'None.').
- Three rejects are pure input-contract violations (integer-only automata
  against 11 x 1/4, 3 / 0.12, 1.1 / 17.6). The pre-draft contract join is
  specified in the task board and blocks these before any model call.
