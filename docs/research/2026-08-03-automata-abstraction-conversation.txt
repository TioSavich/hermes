First, a correction to my own claim, because it matters here: the corpus isn't uniformly scripts. `counting2`'s carry machine, the long-division remainder cycle, the borrow-across-zero cascade, and above all `jason_fsm`'s FCS — which calls PFS as a subroutine and suspends a whole while operating on its part — are real machines with real control structure. Now the Brandom point. In *Between Saying and Doing* the whole apparatus of algorithmic elaboration is stated in automata-theoretic terms — PP-sufficiency is discharged by *exhibiting the elaborating machine*, and the Chomsky hierarchy is what gives "practices-or-abilities" their internal stratification. For `mua_relations.pl`'s claims to be earned rather than asserted, the machine structure has to do work. A chain DFA accepts exactly one word; *anything* is an automaton in that sense, so the current form makes the claim true but vacuous — undischarged commitment. The regularization discharges it four ways:

**Folding restores nondeterminism, and nondeterminism is where the deontics live.** When `column_addition_with_carrying` and its three deformations become one machine, the state after computing a column sum has multiple outgoing edges: propagate the carry, drop it, misread it. That state now *means* something — it is the position in the practice where what must be kept can be lost. States stop being `q_step_3` and become deontic loci. The machine recognizes a family of doings and the diagnosis question becomes "which run is this student on," which is what a recognizer is *for*. This is the single biggest gain: the strategy/deformation pairs, currently your redundancy problem, are exactly the material that turns scripts back into automata when merged.

**Typed quantities make them transducers.** A machine over `q(Value, Unit, Frame)` actually transforms a store rather than narrating that it did. "The doing is represented as an automaton" becomes literal: configuration = control state + quantity store, step = store transformation. That's a register machine or transducer, not a labeled list.

**Hierarchy placement becomes provable — and this is where you get to DO analytic pragmatism rather than gesture at it.** Counting-on is finite-state. Columnar arithmetic with carry is a finite-state transducer. **The ledger split is commitment and entitlement.** Once invariants are acceptance conditions rather than pseudo-actions, every machine has two notions of accepting: reaching q_accept (the doing completed — a commitment undertaken) and keeping the ledger (the doing vindicated — entitlement preserved). A deformation machine *accepts without entitlement*: the student finishes, asserts, and is committed to something they cannot vindicate. Dual acceptance conditions are ordinary automata theory, and they are exactly two-ply scorekeeping. Your Part 3 commitment automata already speak this vocabulary; the regularization makes it one meta-form across the quantitative and discursive genres instead of a comment claiming they rhyme.
