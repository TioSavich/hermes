## Goals

- Comprehend (in spoken language) the meaning of the term 'partial quotients.'
- Explain (orally and in writing) strategies for dividing three-digit numbers by one-digit numbers.
- Interpret (orally and in writing) recording methods for division that uses partial quotients.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is to introduce students to ways to record partial quotients when dividing multi-digit numbers.

## Narrative

Previously, students have found quotients by decomposing a dividend and finding the quotient for each decomposed part until all of the dividend is divided. Students also reasoned in terms of multiplication-adding partial products until they reach the value of the dividend-and in terms of place value. They also used area diagrams and base-ten diagrams-among other representations-to support their reasoning.

In this lesson, students use partial quotients and a couple of ways to record them systematically-by writing a series of equations, and by using an algorithm that uses partial quotients.
