## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for rounding multi-digit whole numbers to the nearest thousand, tenthousand, and hundred-thousand.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to round multi-digit whole numbers within 1,000,000 to the nearest thousand, ten-thousand, and hundred-thousand.

## Narrative

In IM Grade 3, students rounded whole numbers to the nearest ten and hundred. In previous lessons, they worked to find the closest multiples of powers of 10. Students build on this work to round whole numbers to the nearest thousand, ten-thousand, and hundred-thousand. Students revisit the convention of rounding up when a number is exactly halfway between two consecutive multiples of a power of 10.
