## Goals

- Create a Number Talk activity.
- Describe (orally) how a set of expressions are related.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to use patterns, structure, and understanding of the properties of operations to design a series of expressions for a Number Talk.

## Narrative

This lesson provides an opportunity to listen to ways in which students make use of structure and repeated reasoning to design a Number Talk. In the Warm-up, students are given a typical Number Talk, with four expressions, and discuss how the expressions are related. In the first activity, students practice anticipating the different ways someone might reason about addition and subtraction for a group of expressions, and then create expressions that would fit with the given string of expressions. In the final two activities, students are given incomplete sets, with a decreasing number of expressions, and asked to write new expressions to create a Number Talk. This lesson can take 1-2 days if students facilitate their creations with other groups.
