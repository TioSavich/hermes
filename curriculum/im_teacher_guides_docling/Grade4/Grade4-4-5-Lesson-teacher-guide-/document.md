## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for comparing and ordering decimals.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to compare and order fractions and decimals to the hundredths.

## Narrative

In a previous unit, students compared and ordered fractions. Earlier in this unit, they did the same with decimals. In this lesson, students compare and order tenths and hundredths in both fraction and decimal notations.
