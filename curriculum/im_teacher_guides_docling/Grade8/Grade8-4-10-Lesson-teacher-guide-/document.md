## Learning Targets

- I can identify ordered pairs that are solutions to an equation.
- I can interpret ordered pairs that are solutions to an equation.

## Lesson Narrative

In this lesson, students consider pairs of linear equations in familiar contexts, one where there is an equation constraining the possible combinations of two quantities and another where there is an initial value and a rate of change. Students interpret the meaning of points on and off the graphs of the equations. After revisiting the idea that coordinates for points on the graph make the associated equation true, students interpret the intersection point of two lines as a special case in which both equations are true. By examining several points on and off the lines, students make use of repeated reasoning to understand their connection to equations (MP8).
