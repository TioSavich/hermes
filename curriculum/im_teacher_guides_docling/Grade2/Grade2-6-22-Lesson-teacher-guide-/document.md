## Goals

- Determine (orally) the consequences of spending an amount of money in a real-world problem.
- Find the values of amounts of money in a real-world problem and present (using words and other representations) how much is spent, what it was spent on, and how much is left.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to solve problems involving dollar amounts and to make decisions about spending money.

## Narrative

This lesson is optional because it does not address any new mathematical content standards. This lesson does provide students with an opportunity to apply precursor skills of mathematical modeling (MP4).

In previous lessons, students found the values of groups of bills and solved one- and two-step word problems involving money. In this lesson, students continue to solve problems involving dollar amounts in the context of spending money at the fair. Students first look at how someone else spent their money and consider whether or not they should continue to spend or save their money. Then students make decisions about how they would spend their own money. They decide how much they would spend, what they would spend it on, and explain the reasoning for the decisions they made.
