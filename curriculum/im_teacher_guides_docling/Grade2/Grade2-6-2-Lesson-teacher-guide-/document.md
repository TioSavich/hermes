## Goals

- Draw and identify (orally and in writing) shapes that have a given number of sides or corners.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to recognize and draw shapes that have a specific number of sides or corners.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students sorted and identified two-dimensional shapes, based on their sides and corners. In previous grades, students built and drew squares, circles, triangles, rectangles, and hexagons.

In this lesson, students recognize and draw shapes, based on a given number of sides or corners. Students continue to practice shape vocabulary (quadrilateral, pentagon, hexagon) and describe shapes, based on their defining attributes (MP6).
