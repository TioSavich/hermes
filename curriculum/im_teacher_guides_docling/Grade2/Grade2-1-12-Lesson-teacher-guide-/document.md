## Goals

- Ask (orally) and answer questions that can be answered about the data represented in a picture graph or bar graph.
- Represent data using picture graphs and bar graphs.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to practice organizing data and using graphs to represent it. Students also practice adding and subtracting within 10, 20, or 50.

## Narrative

This lesson is optional because it is an opportunity for extra practice that not all classes may need. In the first activity, students learn Stage 2 of the Sort and Display center, which was first introduced in grade 1. In this new stage, called Picture or Bar Graphs, students sort 20-30 objects into 3 categories and make a picture or bar graph that shows how they are sorted. In the second activity, students choose between two previously introduced centers focused on addition and subtraction.
