## Goals

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to add and subtract within 100 as well as tell and solve math stories.

## Narrative

This lesson is optional because it is an opportunity for extra practice that not all classes may need. In the first activity, students learn Stage 4 of the Number Puzzles center. Students work with sums and differences within 100 with composing and decomposing a ten. In the second activity, students choose to continue working on Number Puzzles or on a previously introduced center focused on solving story problems.
