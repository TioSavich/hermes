## Goals

- Determine whether a collection of objects can be arranged into groups of 2 (or 'pairs') with 'one leftover' or 'no leftovers,' and describe (orally) observations about each category.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to pair all of the objects in a group and understand that some numbers of objects can be paired without any objects left over.

## Narrative

In a previous lesson, students determined whether a group of objects could be split into 2 equal groups and discovered that for some numbers of objects, there will be 1 leftover.

In this lesson, students learn that some numbers of objects can be split into multiple groups of 2 (pairs) without any incomplete pairs. In the Lesson Synthesis, students compare the charts from this lesson and the previous lesson that show no leftovers or 1 leftover. They notice that the lists of numbers are the same. The terms even and odd are introduced and added to the charts.

Students should have access to connecting cubes or counters throughout the lesson, including the Cool-down.
