## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for subtracting a two-digit number from a three-digit number, based on place value.
- Explain (orally) when one or more units will be decomposed when subtracting by place.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to analyze expressions to determine if a unit will be decomposed before subtracting.

## Narrative

In a previous lesson, students decomposed a ten or a hundred to subtract three-digit numbers by place. Students represented their thinking with base-ten blocks, drawings, words, or numbers, and explained the steps of their method.

In this lesson, students subtract two-digit numbers from three-digit numbers when two units are decomposed. Students are encouraged to attend to the details of the numbers in each expression to decide whether or not any units will need to be decomposed before subtracting (MP7). Throughout the lesson, students explain their reasoning and critique their peers' reasoning as they use their understanding of place value to analyze expressions and plan their methods (MP3).
