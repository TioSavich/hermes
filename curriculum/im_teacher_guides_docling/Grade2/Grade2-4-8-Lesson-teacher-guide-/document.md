## Goals

- Create equations to represent number line diagrams that show addition or subtraction.
- Represent addition and subtraction on a number line diagram.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to write equations based on number lines and to represent equations on a number line.

## Narrative

In the previous lesson, students interpreted representations of addition and subtraction equations on a number line. They used the direction of the arrow to determine what operation was represented and matched equations to number lines.

In this lesson, students write equations based on number line representations and represent given equations on number lines.
