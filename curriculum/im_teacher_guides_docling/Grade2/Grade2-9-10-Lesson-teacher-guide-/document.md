## Goals

- Ask (orally and in writing) a question that can be answered based on given information.
- Explain (orally) strategies for representing and solving story problems.
- Interpret (orally and in writing) diagrams and equations that represent a solution to a story problem.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to analyze a tape diagram and number line diagram and determine, in context, a question that could be answered based on each representation.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students sorted and solved a variety of story problems. In this lesson, students use given information to ask math questions and figure out what question was asked when presented with student work. Students interpret the context of a story and analyze tape diagrams to determine what question is being asked (MP2, MP4). Students then pose a math question and use a representation of their choice to answer it.
