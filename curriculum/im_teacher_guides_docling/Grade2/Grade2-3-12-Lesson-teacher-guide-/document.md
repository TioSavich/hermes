## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for representing and solving two-step Take From and Compare problems about length.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to represent and solve two-step story problems. Students use representations to make sense of problems, support their calculations, and explain their thinking.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students represented and solved one- and two-step story problems in a way that made sense to them. They have used tape diagrams to represent comparisons and part-part-whole relationships.

Since students are familiar with centimeters and inches, the stories in this lesson include both. In the first activity, students make sense of and solve a two-step problem where both parts are Take From, Result Unknown. In the second activity, they work together to make sense of two-step problems that include more challenging problem types, including two-step problems that might require using two different operations.
