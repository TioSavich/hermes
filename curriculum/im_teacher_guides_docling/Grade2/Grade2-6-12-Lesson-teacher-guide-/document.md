## Goals

- Determine (orally and in writing) the time on a clock to the nearest 5 minutes, and explain (orally) a strategy for telling time.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to tell time to the nearest 5 minutes on an analog clock.

## Narrative

In grade 1, students learned to tell time to the hour and half hour. In a previous lesson, students learned to tell time, using the phrases 'half past,' 'quarter past,' and 'quarter till,' and described the relative position of the hour hand.

In this lesson, students make connections between a number line with intervals of 5 and the distance between the numbers on the clock, which represents 5-minute intervals. Students skip-count by 5 in order to tell time.

## Standards

Addressing 2.MD.C.7, 2.NBT.A.2

The purpose of this activity is for students to practice counting by 5 in order to tell time on an analog clock. In the Launch, students practice using what they notice in the Warm-up to tell time to the nearest 5 minutes on an analog clock. Then they work with partners to tell the time on analog clocks and to sequence their cards in time order. During the Activity Synthesis, students discuss how they use the labeled numbers on the clock to determine the time as well as what they notice about how the hour hand moves as time passes in minutes.
