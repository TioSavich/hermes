## Goals

- Match (orally and in writing) Compare or Put Together/Take Apart problems and tape diagrams that represent the same relationships.
- Represent and solve story problems within 100.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to solve story problems of different problem types within 100. Students interpret tape diagrams and connect them to different types of story problems.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students solved different story problems within 50 and compared different diagrams and methods. Students interpreted and used tape diagrams to represent Compare story problems.

The problems in this lesson include some of the more challenging types, such as Add To, Start Unknown. Students are introduced to tape diagrams as a way to represent the known and unknown quantities in Add To and Put Together/Take Apart problem types. Students are encouraged to find the unknown values in the way that makes the most sense to them. Students have opportunities to practice composing and decomposing a ten when using strategies based on adding or subtracting by place.

## Launch

- Groups of 2
- Give each group a set of cards.
- Give each group access to base-ten blocks.

## Activity

- 'This set of cards includes story problems and diagrams.'
- 'You are going to take turns reading a story problem. After one person reads, match the story problem to a diagram. Work with your partner to explain your reasoning. Then pick a new story problem and find a diagram that matches.'
- 'If it helps, you may label the diagrams to explain your matches.'
- As needed, demonstrate the activity with a student volunteer.
- 'When your group finishes, choose 2 story problems from Story Problems A, B, C, or D and solve them.'
- 8 minutes: small-group work time
- Monitor for students who explain how each diagram matches the quantities in the context of the story problem.
- 4 minutes: independent work time
- Monitor for students who solve Story Problem B or C using methods based on place value.
