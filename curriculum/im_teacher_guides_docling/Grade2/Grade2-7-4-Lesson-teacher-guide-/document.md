## Goals

- Compare and contrast (orally) approaches for adding and subtracting within 1,000 that do not include composing or decomposing units when adding by place.
- Use different strategies and recording methods to find the values of sums and differences within 1,000.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to add and subtract within 1,000, without composing a ten or a hundred, using place-value understanding, properties of operations, and the relationship between addition and subtraction.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students used what they know about counting within 1,000 and place value to subtract multiples of 10 and 100 from three-digit numbers. In this lesson, students continue to apply methods they used when adding and subtracting within 100 to add and subtract within 1,000. They also make sense of methods represented with equations. In the first activity, students make sense of different methods for subtracting a three-digit number from a multiple of 100. In the second activity, they make sense of methods based on adding by place. Students are invited to try the methods for adding and subtracting that they analyze in this lesson or to choose any method that makes the most sense to them. They will continue to analyze and use methods based on adding and subtracting by place in upcoming lessons.
