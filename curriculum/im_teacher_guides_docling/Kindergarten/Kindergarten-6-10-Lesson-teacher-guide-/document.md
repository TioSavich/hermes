## Goals

- Use equations to represent numbers 11-19 as a sum of 10 and some more ones.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to represent numbers 11-19 with equations.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students composed and decomposed numbers 11-19 with 10 ones and some more ones. They saw numbers 11-19 represented with written numbers, phrases such as '10 and 2,' expressions, and equations. In this lesson, students interpret equations and fill in numbers to complete equations for numbers 11-19 (MP2).

This lesson has a Student Section Summary.
