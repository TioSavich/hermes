## Goals

- Explain (orally) methods for identifying the value of addition and subtraction expressions.
- Explain (orally) strategies for comparing addition and subtraction expressions.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to develop fluency with adding and subtracting within 5.

## Narrative

Students move around the room as they find the values of expressions. Students also compare totals and differences of expressions.

If students need additional support with the concepts in this lesson, refer back to Unit 4, Section C in the curriculum materials.

## Activity 2

Compare Expressions

![Image](document_artifacts/image_000028_71650d589bde798eefe845d7dc379b6da7e842ef99331f5f2a66dee499b140d1.png)

## Standards

Addressing

K.CC.C.6

The purpose of this activity is for students to compare totals or differences of expressions. Students may add or subtract to find the value or they may decide which expression is greater, based on reasoning about operations or the number sequence (MP7). For example, students know that 4+1 is greater than 4-3 because one expression is adding to 4 and the other is taking away from 4. As a variation, the student whose card has an expression with a lesser value takes both cards.
