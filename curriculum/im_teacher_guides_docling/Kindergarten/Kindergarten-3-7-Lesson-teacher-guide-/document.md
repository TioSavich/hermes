## Goals

- Determine if straws are longer or shorter than a given line.
- Use the terms 'longer than' and 'shorter than' to compare (orally) the lengths of two objects.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to build shapes from components.

## Narrative

In a previous lesson, students compared the lengths of rectangles that were lined up. In this lesson, students work with straws to build shapes. Students initially compare the lengths of the straws, and then they use the straws to build shapes. If straws are not available, other materials may be used as long as there are at least four different lengths of the material.
