## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for comparing written numbers.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to compare numbers 1-10.

## Narrative

Students can compare numbers in any way that makes sense to them. Students may:

- Create groups of objects or draw groups of images and match to compare.
- Use the count sequence to compare.
- Begin to automatically know which numbers are less or more than a given number.
