## Goals

- Create an expression and a story problem that represent the same relationships.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to consider the information needed to solve story problems and to create a story problem to match an expression.

## Narrative

This lesson is optional because it does not address any new mathematical content standards. This lesson does provide students with an opportunity to apply precursor skills of mathematical modeling (MP4).

In previous lessons, students solved story problems and matched expressions to story problems. In the first activity, students consider a story problem that includes additional information and determine which information is necessary to answer the question. Then students tell their own story problem to match an expression. Students think about what information they do and do not need to include when telling their story problem.

If there is additional time, students can participate in centers from the unit.
