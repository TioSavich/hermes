## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for determining '1 more' or '1 less' than a given number.
- Generalize (orally) that adding 1 more results in the next number in the count sequence and taking away 1 results in the previous number in the count sequence.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to use their knowledge of the count sequence to determine 1 more and 1 less than a number or a group of objects.

## Narrative

This lesson is optional, because the standards do not require students to compare groups of objects or written numbers beyond 10. This lesson does give students more practice with counting out a given number and determining 1 more and 1 less.

In a previous lesson, students used their knowledge of the count sequence to solve story problems involving adding and subtracting one. In this lesson, students apply this understanding to determine 1 more and 1 less than a given number or group of objects, without a context. Students practice counting out a given number of objects. When determining 1 more or 1 less, students may recount their collection from 1 or may use their knowledge of the count sequence to know what 1 more or 1 less is.

If students need additional support with the concepts in this lesson, refer back to Unit 2, Section D in the curriculum materials.
