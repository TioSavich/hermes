## Goals

- Comprehend (in spoken language) the terms 'above,' 'below,' 'next to,' and 'behind' are used to describe the location of objects.
- Describe (orally) the location of shapes, using 'above,' 'below,' 'next to,' and 'behind.'

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to use positional words to describe the location of pattern blocks in a larger shape.

## Narrative

While some students may already be familiar with the positional words, students are formally introduced to the words above , below , next to , and beside . Students hear and use these words to describe the location of objects in the classroom. Students describe shapes made out of pattern blocks and use positional words to help their partner create the same shape. Listen to how students create an argument and use or revise language to make their argument clear (MP3, MP6).

## Standards

Addressing

K.G.A.1

The purpose of this activity is for students to learn Stage 1 of the Match Mine center. One student makes an object, using pattern blocks and without letting their partner see. They use positional words to describe the location of pattern blocks within the object, and then their partner tries to make the same object based on the description. Students may initially describe the objects generally or use positional words other than those introduced in the previous activity. Students need repeated experiences hearing positional words in order to develop precision with their language (MP6).

Folders or other objects can be used to keep students' work hidden.
