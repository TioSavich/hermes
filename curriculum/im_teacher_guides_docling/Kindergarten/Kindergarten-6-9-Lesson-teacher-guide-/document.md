## Goals

- Match (orally) equations and images of 10-frames that represent the same relationships.
- Match (orally) expressions and numbers that represent the same value.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to make sense of expressions and equations that represent numbers 11-19.

## Narrative

Students connect their understanding of numbers as 10 ones and some more ones to expressions in the form 10 + \_\_\_\_\_. Then students match equations to 10-frame representations of teen numbers.
