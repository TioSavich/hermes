## Goals

- Comprehend (in spoken language) the term 'subtract' refers to taking away.
- Repeat (orally) statements in the form '\_\_\_\_\_ take away \_\_\_\_\_ . There are \_\_\_\_\_ left.' to represent subtraction statements.
- Represent and solve subtraction problems.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to represent the action of subtraction with objects.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students represented addition by putting 2 groups of objects together or adding more objects. In this lesson, students represent the action of subtraction with objects. Students take away a number of objects and determine the number of objects remaining. The subtraction language of 'take away' and ' subtract ' is introduced by the teacher throughout the lesson. Students are not expected to produce this language in this lesson. The notation '\_\_\_\_\_ take away \_\_\_\_\_' is also introduced. Students need time to see and hear the teacher use this language before they can understand expressions '\_\_\_\_\_ - \_\_\_\_\_,' which are introduced in a later section.
