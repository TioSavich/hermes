## Goals

- Create designs composed of 2 types of pattern blocks. Describe (orally) this composition using number names for each part and the total.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to compose and decompose numbers up to 9 in more than 1 way.

## Narrative

In a previous unit, students made designs with pattern blocks and counted how many of each pattern block they used. In this lesson, students make and share a design with the same total number of pattern blocks but different numbers of individual pattern blocks.
