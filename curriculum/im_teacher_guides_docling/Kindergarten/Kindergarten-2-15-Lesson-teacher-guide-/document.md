## Goals

- Determine (orally) the number of images presented in lines, arrays, and circles, on fingers, and on 5-frames.
- Match (orally) written numbers and groups of images.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to connect groups of images in different arrangements to spoken and written numbers.

## Narrative

In a previous section, students counted groups of images in lines, arrays, and dot cube arrangements, on 5-frames, and on fingers. In this lesson, students first match one group of images with a number. Several of these groups have images in a circle. Circular arrangements can be more challenging for students because they need to keep track of where they started counting. Students need repeated practice counting and keeping track of images arranged in circles. This skill will be revisited in future units and is not necessary for all students by the end of this lesson.

In the second activity, students match more than one group of images to each number. This encourages students to notice that the arrangement of a group of images does not affect the number of images.

Two-color counters and connecting cubes should be made available to students. As demonstrated in the optional activity in the previous section, students can place one object on each image as they count as one way to keep track.

Students have used sets of cards in the Fewer, Same, More Stages 2 and 3 blackline master in previous sections and lessons. Add the cards from the Different Arrangements, Same Number Cards blackline master to the existing sets of cards.

In the Lesson Synthesis, students practice saying the verbal count sequence to 20. Add variety to the counting by using movement. For example, students can count as they clap, stomp their feet, or jump.
