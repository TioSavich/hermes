## Goals

- Create drawings to represent a group of objects in the school community and represent the group with a written number.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to use numbers to represent objects in their environment.

## Narrative

In a previous lesson, students explored number books and brainstormed important objects and features of their school community to include in a number book. In this lesson, students create, revise, and share their own number books about their school community. When students represent objects in their school with pictures and numbers, they reason abstractly and quantitatively (MP2).

If students need additional support with the concepts in this lesson, refer back to Unit 2, Section C in the curriculum materials.
