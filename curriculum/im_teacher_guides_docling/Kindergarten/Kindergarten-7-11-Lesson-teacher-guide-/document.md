## Goals

- Compare and contrast (orally) solid shapes, using informal language.
- Describe (orally) the attributes of solid shapes, using informal language.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to compare solid shapes.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students identified, described, and built solid shapes. In this lesson, students describe and compare the attributes of solid shapes. When describing and comparing shapes, students are not required to know or use language such as 'faces,' 'edges,' or 'vertices.' Students may use their own language to describe these attributes, such as 'sides,' 'points,' or 'corners.' If it is unclear to which part of a shape a student is referring, consider asking them to point to it.
