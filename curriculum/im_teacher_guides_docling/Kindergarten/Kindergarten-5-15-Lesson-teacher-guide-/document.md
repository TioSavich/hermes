## Goals

- Present (orally and using other representations) compositions and decompositions within 10 that represents a solution to a real-world context.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to compose and decompose numbers to 10 and represent situations involving compositions and decompositions of numbers to 10.

## Narrative

This lesson is optional because it does not address any new mathematical content standards. This lesson does provide students with an opportunity to apply precursor skills of mathematical modeling (MP4).

In previous lessons, students composed and decomposed numbers to 10, both in the context of story problems and using math tools. In this lesson, students choose how to pack a box with two different types of fruit. Then they write an expression to represent how many of each fruit is packed in the box.

If there is additional time, students can participate in centers from the unit.

## Activity Synthesis

- Choose one student's display to share with the class. Do not show the display, but write an expression that shows how they packed the box. For example, if the student put 1 apple and 6 pears in their box, display and write .
- 'I wrote this expression about the box of fruit that \_\_\_\_\_ packed. What do we know about how \_\_\_\_\_ packed the box?' (We know that they packed the box with 2 kinds of fruit. We know that they put 1 of one kind of fruit and 6 of another kind of fruit. We know that they put 7 fruits in the box altogether.)
- 'What questions do you still have about \_\_\_\_\_\_'s box?' (What kind of fruit did they put in the box? How many of each fruit did they put in the box?)
- Invite the previously identified student to share their display. Ask students how the numbers in the expression connect to the display.
- Repeat with as many students as time allows.
