## Goals

- Interpret (orally) a subtraction expression that represents a Take From, Result Unknown problem.
- Match (orally) expressions and Add To or Take, From, Result Unknown problems.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to connect expressions to story problems.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students were introduced to addition and subtraction expressions as another way to represent story problems. In this lesson, students explain how expressions connect to story problems and choose which expression correctly represents a story problem (MP2).
