## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to develop story problems about their school community.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students represented objects in number books and answered mathematical questions about their school community. In this lesson, students develop story problems about their school community. In the next lesson, students will share and solve their story problems.

If students need additional support with the concepts in this lesson, refer back to Unit 5, Section B in the curriculum materials.
