## Goals

- Explain (orally) approaches for ordering numbers from 1 to 10.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to order numbers from 1 to 10.

## Narrative

Students build cube towers to match each number from 1 to 10. They put the towers and numbers in order. Students may use the count sequence to put the numbers in order, or they may use the cube towers as representations of each number to help them put the numbers in order. This representation of the numbers and cube towers for each number will be used in the next lesson as students determine 1 more and 1 less than a given number.

During this lesson and throughout the section, students who are not yet recognizing written numbers should have access to the Numbers 1 -10 with 5-Frames Reference Sheet blackline master.

## Launch

## Activity

- 5 minutes: partner work time
- As you monitor for the approaches listed in the Activity Narrative, consider asking:
- 'How did you order your number cards?'
- 'How did you order your towers?'
- 'Why did you order your \_\_\_\_\_ first?'
- 'How do you know the towers match the number cards?'

## Activity Synthesis

- Invite previously selected students to share in order from the most common approach to the least common approach. Record or display their work.
- Connect students' approaches by asking:
- 'What is the same about how they ordered their cards and towers?' (They both have cards like we count, 1-10. They both have the towers from smallest to biggest.)
- 'What is different about how they ordered their cards and towers?' (One started with the number cards. One started with the cube towers. One counted the cubes, and one ordered by height.)
- Connect students' approaches to the learning goal by asking:
- 'How did it help you to put the towers in order first?' (We started with the smallest tower that only had 1 cube, and then we kept finding the tower that had 1 more cube. Then we could just match the numbers to the towers.)
- 'How did it help you to put the numbers in order first?' (We put the numbers in order from 1 to 10 like when we count. Then we picked up each tower, counted the cubes, and put it next to the number it matches.)
