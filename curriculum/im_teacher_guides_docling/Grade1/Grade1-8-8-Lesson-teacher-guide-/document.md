## Goals

- Compare and contrast (orally) different base-ten representations of the same two-digit number.
- Represent the same two-digit number in different ways.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to represent two-digit numbers in different ways to demonstrate place value understanding.

## Narrative

Students represent a two-digit number in as many ways as they can. They are encouraged to think about representations of their number that have different amounts of tens. Then they do a Gallery Walk to compare representations their classmates made.

If students need additional support with the concepts in this lesson, refer back to Unit 4, Section D in the curriculum materials.
