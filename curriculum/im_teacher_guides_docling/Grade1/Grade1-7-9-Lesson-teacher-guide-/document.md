## Goals

- Comprehend (in spoken and written language) the meaning of the terms 'halves' and 'fourths.'
- Explain (orally) how to partition a shape into equalsize pieces, including halves and fourths.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to partition circles and rectangles into halves and fourths and to determine whether a shape is partitioned into equal pieces.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students created two- and three-dimensional shapes from smaller shapes.

Students begin this lesson by composing shapes, and then they compare shapes made with equal pieces to those made with unequal pieces. They partition shapes into two and four equal pieces and learn the terms halves and fourths . Students do not have to create exactly equal pieces, but they should be able to explain that the pieces are supposed to be equal.
