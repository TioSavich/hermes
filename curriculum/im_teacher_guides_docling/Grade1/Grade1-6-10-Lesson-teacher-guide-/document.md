## Goals

- Choose and use length units to estimate and measure the length of objects.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to practice measuring and adding within 100.

## Narrative

Students learn a new center called Estimate and Measure, where they choose an object and a length unit to measure it with. They estimate the length of the object before measuring to find the actual length. Students then choose from previously introduced centers that focus on adding within 100.

## Launch

- Groups of 2
- Give each group access to objects they can measure as well as various measuring units. Give each student a copy of the blackline master.
- 'We are going to learn a new center called Estimate and Measure- Choose Your Unit. In this activity, you choose an object that you will measure. Then you choose the unit you would like to use to measure the object. Record the object and the unit you will use to measure. Before measuring the object, make an estimate of the object's length. Record your estimate. Then measure the object and record the actual measurement.'

## Activity Synthesis

- Display an object measured with gaps between some of the length units.

- Display an object measured with different length units such as centimeter cubes and connecting cubes.
- 'What feedback could you give to someone who measured like this?' (You have to use the same object to measure the entire length of the object. You can't use small and big cubes, you have to choose one.)
