## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for adding two-digit numbers and a multiple of 10.
- Find the digit that makes an equation representing adding two-digit numbers and a multiple of 10 true.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to add a two-digit number and a multiple of 10.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students added and subtracted multiples of 10 from other multiples of 10. In this lesson, students reason about the value of expressions where both addends are two-digit numbers-one addend is a multiple of 10 and the other is not. Students use and explain methods that make sense to them and show their understanding about what the two digits in a two-digit number represent (MP3, MP7).
