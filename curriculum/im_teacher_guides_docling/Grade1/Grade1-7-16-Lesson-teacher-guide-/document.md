## Goals

- Critique (orally and using words or other representations) arguments about how to read the time on an analog clock.
- Interpret and represent times to the hour and the half hour, using digital and analog clocks.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to show and write the time in hours and half hours.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students wrote times based on analog clocks showing time in hours and half hours.

In this lesson, students continue to tell time with clocks, paying special attention to times when they may mix-up the minute and hour hands (for example, 12:30 and 6:00). Students also practice writing times and drawing the hands on the clock as they create their ideal Sunday schedule.

This lesson has a Student Section Summary.
