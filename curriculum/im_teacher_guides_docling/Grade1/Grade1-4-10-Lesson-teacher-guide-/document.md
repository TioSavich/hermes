## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to write numbers to represent different base-ten representations including base-ten diagrams, \_\_\_\_\_ tens \_\_\_\_\_ ones, and expressions that show the value of the tens and ones digits.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students read two-digit numbers. Students also interpreted and created representations of twodigit numbers. In this lesson, students write two-digit numbers to match different base-ten representations. Students also learn a new center that provides practice counting and writing numbers up to 99.

## Activity 2

Introduce Write Numbers-Numbers to 99 by 1

![Image](document_artifacts/image_000033_e3e2e022c37b0c2ef8940ab8f88789c08aeab85179e7827af71f40d191a28383.png)

## Standards

Addressing 1.NBT.A.1

The purpose of this activity is for students to learn a new center called Write Numbers. Students choose to count forwards or backwards by starting at either of the two-digit numbers on a gameboard. They take turns counting by 1 and writing the next one, two, or three numbers in the sequence. The player who writes the last number on the gameboard wins.

Consider laminating gameboards or placing them in sheet protectors so they can be used multiple times.
