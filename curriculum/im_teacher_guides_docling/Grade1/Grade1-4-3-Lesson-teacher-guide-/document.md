## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to add and subtract 10 from multiples of 10.

## Narrative

In this lesson, students build on their understanding of tens as they add 10 and subtract 10 from multiples of 10. Students learn to think of adding or subtracting 10 as adding or subtracting a ten from a number of other tens. In the first activity, students use towers of 10 to physically add or subtract a ten from a multiple of 10. In the second activity, students learn a new stage of the Five in a Row center. Students choose a card with a multiple of 10 and decide whether to add or subtract 10. The goal is to cover five spaces in a row on their gameboard. They can continue to use towers of 10 if helpful.

## Activity 2

Introduce Five in a Row-Add or Subtract 10

![Image](document_artifacts/image_000032_c68b09ca8fc5a8b391776c1c794d107426f1c366566ab427360aad06b2bdbb6b.png)

## Standards

Addressing

1.NBT.C.4, 1.NBT.C.5

The purpose of this activity is for students to learn Stage 4 of the Five in a Row center. Students choose a card that shows a multiple of 10 and decide to add or subtract 10 from the number on their card. They then cover the sum or difference on the gameboard with a counter. If students can't make a number that is left on the gameboard, they pick another card. The game ends when a player gets 5 counters in a row or the board is filled.

The gameboard is provided as a blackline master. It will be used again in upcoming lessons. Consider copying it on cardstock or laminating it for future use.
