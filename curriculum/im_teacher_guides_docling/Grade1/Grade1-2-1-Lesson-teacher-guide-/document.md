## Goals

- Represent the actions of Add To and Take From, Result Unknown problems in different ways.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to represent and solve Add To and Take From, Result Unknown story problems in a way that makes sense to them.

## Narrative

Students revisit these familiar problem types and the different ways the actions of the story can be represented. In the process, students reveal what they understand about the operations of addition and subtraction. In these problem types, the quantities can physically be joined or separated. This action leads to the resulting quantity. When students connect their representations and what they understand about addition and subtraction back to the story problem and explain the connections, they model with mathematics (MP4).

In the Lesson Synthesis, students share different ways they can represent a story problem. Collect student ideas on chart paper and save the paper for reference in future lessons.

## Activity

- Read the story with numbers (second problem).
- 'Let's act this story out. We need some actors to be the children listening to the story at the beginning.'
- 'How many actors do we need?' (6)
- Invite 6 students to act out listening to a story.

- Invite 4 more students to act out coming to the library to listen.
- 'How many children are listening to the story now?' (10 children)
- As needed, repeat acting out the story again with a different group of students.
- Repeat the steps for the next story problem.

## Activity Synthesis

- Reread the story about 6 children listening to a story.
- 'What did we do first to act out this story?' (First, 6 of us acted like we were listening.)
- 'What did we do next to act out this story?' (Then 4 more people came and pretended to listen.)
- 'How did you know that 4 people should join the group?' (Because in the story, 4 more kids joined.)
- 'The words in a story help us figure out what is happening. If you imagine what is happening in the story, it can help us solve the problem.'
