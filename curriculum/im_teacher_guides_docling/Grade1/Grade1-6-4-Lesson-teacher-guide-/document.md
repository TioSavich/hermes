## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is to practice adding and subtracting within 100.

## Narrative

In the first activity, students learn Stage 3 of the How Close? center. In this new stage, students add 2 two-digit numbers, trying to get as close to 100 as possible. In the second activity, students choose from previously introduced stages of centers focused on two-digit numbers.

## Launch

- Groups of 2
- Give each group a set of number cards, 2 recording sheets, and access to connecting cubes in towers of 10 and singles.
- 'We are going to learn a new way to play How Close? Let's play one round together.'
- Tell students to remove any card that shows the number 10 and explain they will not use those cards in this activity.
- 'First, we each take seven cards.'
- Display seven cards for you and seven for the class.
- 'Then we each choose four numbers to make 2 twodigit numbers. We want to choose numbers that will have a sum as close to 100 as possible without going over.'
- Demonstrate making 2-digit numbers with four cards and invite students to share what two-digit numbers they would make from the student class cards.
- 'Now we both add our numbers and write an equation so we can see who has a sum closer to 100.'
- Demonstrate adding, writing equations, and comparing the numbers.
- 'The person with the sum closer to 100 gets one point. Then you each choose four more cards so that you have seven cards to start your next round. The person who gets more points wins.'
