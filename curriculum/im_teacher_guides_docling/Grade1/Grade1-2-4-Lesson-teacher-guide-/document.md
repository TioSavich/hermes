## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to interpret a strategy for solving an Add To, Change Unknown problem that involves using drawings to count on. Students use strategies that make sense to them to determine the number that makes an equation true.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students represented Add To story problems including problems where the change was unknown. Students compared different ways to determine an unknown, including counting on. In this lesson, students make sense of a way to represent counting on from one number to another in the context of a Add To, Change Unknown story problem. Students are then invited to use strategies that make sense to them to determine the number that makes an equation true.

In this lesson, students revisit the meaning of an equation, which is first introduced in IM Kindergarten as a way to represent story problems when all quantities are known. Students are not expected to write equations on their own until Section D of this unit, but students may begin to try writing equations on their own before then.

The purpose of this Number Talk is to elicit strategies and understandings students have for finding the value of expressions when an addend is 1, 2, or 3. Because students have been working with Add To, Result Unknown and Add To, Change Unknown stories, they are likely to share strategies based on counting on from the first addend, which will be helpful as they consider ways to make sense of and solve story problems in this unit. ## Activity

- 'Find the number that makes each equation true. You can use objects, drawings, numbers, or words to show your thinking.'
- 4-5 minutes: independent work time
- Consider asking:
- 'Can you show me how you found the number to make this equation true?'
- 'Now share your thinking with your partner. If you disagree, work together to use objects or drawings to figure out which number makes the equation true.'
- 3-4 minutes: partner discussion
- Monitor for students who:
- Use objects or drawings to show counting on from one addend to the total.
- Use their fingers or other methods to count on orally and determine the unknown addend without representing all of the objects.

## Activity Synthesis

- Invite 2-3 previously selected students to share how they found the unknown addend.
- 'How are their methods the same? How are they different?'
- 'What were some other ways you figured out how to make the equations true?'
