## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for adding 2 two-digit numbers within 100, that include composing a ten.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to add 2 two-digit numbers within 100 in any way that makes sense to them, including composing a ten.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students added a one-digit number and a two-digit number with composing a ten. They also added 2 two-digit numbers without composing a new ten. They discussed approaches based on place value and the properties of operations. They wrote equations to show their thinking.

In this lesson, students add 2 two-digit numbers in any way that makes sense to them. Students may apply methods learned in previous lessons, including approaches based on making a new ten or adding tens and tens and ones and ones. Students are not required to write equations, although some may do so.
