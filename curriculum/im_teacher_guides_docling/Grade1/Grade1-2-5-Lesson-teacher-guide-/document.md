The purpose of this lesson is for students to tell and solve story problems, and add and subtract within 10.

In Activity 1, students pose and solve addition and subtraction story problems based on pictures. During Center Choice Time, students choose an activity to work on that focuses on addition and subtraction within 10.

The purpose of this Warm-up is to introduce the students to a new routine called True or False? that will be used throughout the year. The purpose of this True or False? is to give students an opportunity to deepen their understanding of the equal sign. Because equations are less familiar to students, the teacher should read aloud each equation. When students are more familiar with equations, they will read the equations themselves. In the Activity Synthesis, students are asked to reword another student's thinking and justify the answer in a different way (MP3).
