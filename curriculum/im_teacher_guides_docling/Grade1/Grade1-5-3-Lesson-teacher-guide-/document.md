## Goals

- Interpret (using words or other representations) equations that represent steps for adding based on place value.
- Represent strategies for adding 2 two-digit numbers with equations and explain (orally and using words or other representations) the strategies.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to add 2 two-digit numbers, without composing a ten, using methods based on place value and to write equations to represent addition methods.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students used methods based on place value to add within 100, without composing a ten. They analyzed equations that represent methods for finding the sum.

In this lesson, students add two-digit numbers using methods of their choice and write equations to match their thinking. Students interpret and compare different methods for finding the value of the same sums. Students also practice explaining their own methods and listening to the methods of their peers. Students have opportunities to revise how they explain their own and others' methods and consider how representations of their own thinking (for example, drawings or equations) can help them explain or interpret their work (MP3, MP6).
