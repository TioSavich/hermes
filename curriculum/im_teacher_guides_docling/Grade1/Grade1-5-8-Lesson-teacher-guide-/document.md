## Activity 1

Introduce Target Numbers-Add Tens or Ones

![Image](document_artifacts/image_000016_adee0c4f0052d5ed460de267d9334c8c993fb1d6349853344b5027233c39788c.png)

## Standards

Addressing 1.NBT.C.4

The purpose of this activity is for students to learn Stage 2 of the Target Numbers center. Students add tens or ones to get as close to 95 as possible. Students take turns picking a number card, deciding to add that number of tens or ones to their starting number for the round, and writing an equation. Students start their first equation at 25 and then the sum becomes the first addend in the next round. The player who gets closest to 95 in 6 rounds, without going over, is the winner.
