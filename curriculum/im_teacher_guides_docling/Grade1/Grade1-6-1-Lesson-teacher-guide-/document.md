## Goals

- Explain (orally) a strategy for comparing the length of two objects and ordering up to three objects by length.
- Use 'longer than' and 'shorter than' to compare (orally and in writing) the length of up to three objects.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to compare the lengths of objects by lining up the endpoints and to order objects by length.

## Narrative

In kindergarten, students compared the lengths of two objects directly by lining up the endpoints. They described the objects using such language as "longer" and "shorter." In this unit, the words 'longer than' and 'shorter than' are encouraged, although students may use 'taller than' in certain contexts related to height.

In this lesson, students compare the lengths of objects and consider how they know which is longer or shorter. Then they order three objects by length.

The materials used in this lesson will be used again in the next lesson.
