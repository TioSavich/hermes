## Goals

- Describe (orally) a two-dimensional shape and draw a shape, based on the given description.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to work with shapes and numbers.

## Narrative

In the first activity, students learn Stage 1 of the Can You Draw It? center. In this center, students choose a shape card and describe the shape to their partner, who tries to draw the shape, based on the description. In the second activity, students choose from previously introduced centers that focus on shapes, addition, or subtraction.

## Introduce Can You Draw It-Grade 1 Shapes

![Image](document_artifacts/image_000026_e3e2e022c37b0c2ef8940ab8f88789c08aeab85179e7827af71f40d191a28383.png)

## Standards

Addressing

1.G.A.1

The purpose of this activity is for students to learn Stage 1 of the Can You Draw It? center. Students describe and draw two-dimensional shapes. One student secretly picks a shape card and describes the shape to their partner, who draws it, based on the description. Together they compare the shape that was drawn to the shape on the card. If the student has drawn the shape correctly, they keep the card. The partner with the most cards at the end of the game wins. Before playing, remove any cards that do not show a triangle or quadrilateral.

- 'How can we describe this shape?' (It has 4 sides. The top and the bottom are longer than the sides. It has square corners.)
- 'When your partner is done drawing the shape, show them the card. If your partner has drawn the correct shape, they keep the card. Take turns describing and drawing shapes. The person with the most cards at the end of the game wins.'
