## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for finding the number that makes equations with two-digit numbers true.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to practice adding within 100 without composing a ten.

## Narrative

In the first activity, students learn Stage 3 of the Number Puzzles center. In this new stage, students use digit cards to make addition equations within 100 without composing a ten. In the second activity, students choose from center activities that focus on adding within 100.

## Introduce Number Puzzles-Within 100 without Composing

![Image](document_artifacts/image_000023_e3e2e022c37b0c2ef8940ab8f88789c08aeab85179e7827af71f40d191a28383.png)

## Standards

Addressing

1.NBT.C.4, 1.OA.D.8

The purpose of this activity is for students to learn Stage 2 of the Number Puzzles center. Students work together to use digit cards to make addition and subtraction equations true. Students add and subtract within 100 without composing. Each digit (0-9) can only be used once in a puzzle. Puzzles with less than ten spaces will have leftover cards.

## Required Materials

## Materials to Gather

- Materials from previous centers: Activity 1

## Required Preparation

- Each group of 2 needs one set of digits (0-9) from the Number Puzzle Digit Cards from a previous stage of this center.

## Launch

- Groups of 2
- Give each group a set of digits (0-9) and a set of gameboards.
- 'We are going to learn a new way to play Number Puzzles.'
- 'These number puzzles have addition equations with 2-digit numbers. Use the digit cards to make each equation true. Remember, you can only use each digit card once on the gameboard and some puzzles will have leftover cards.'

## Activity

- 10 minutes: partner work time

## Activity Synthesis

- Display a gameboard.
- 'Which equation can you fill in first?'
