## Goals

- Describe (orally) and identify two-digit numbers using clues based on place value.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to use place value understanding to compare and write numbers.

## Narrative

In the first activity, students learn a new center called Mystery Number. In this center, students give clues to help their partner guess a two-digit number. In the second activity, students choose between center activities introduced earlier in the unit.

## Standards

Addressing 1.NBT.B

The purpose of this activity is for students to learn a new center called Mystery Number. One student picks 2 cards and makes a two-digit number without showing their partner. They give clues about the mystery number based on the sentence starters and their partner guesses the mystery number. Students continue going back and forth giving clues and guessing the number until the mystery number is identified. Then students switch roles. Students earn 1 point for each clue they get to help them identify the mystery number. The player with the lowest score after 5 rounds wins.

## Launch

- Groups of 2
- Give each group a set of cards and directions.
- 'We are going to learn a new center called Mystery Number. Remove the cards that show 10."
- 'On your turn, you will choose 2 number cards and make a 2-digit number without showing your partner. Give your partner a clue about your mystery number. You can use one of the sentence starters or make up your own clues. If your partner guesses your mystery number, switch roles. If they don't guess the mystery number, give them another clue. Continue giving clues until they guess your number. You get 1 point for each clue you used to help you identify the mystery number. The person with the lowest score after 5 rounds wins.'
