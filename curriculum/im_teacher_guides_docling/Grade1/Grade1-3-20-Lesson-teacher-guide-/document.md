## Goals

- Explain (orally) strategies for solving Put Together/ Take Apart, Total Unknown problems with three addends.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to solve story problems with three addends.

## Narrative

In this lesson, students solve story problems that call for addition of three numbers. Students solve the problems in any way that makes sense to them. Students may work flexibly with different methods based on the numbers they are adding.
