## Goals

- Interpret (orally) addition equations with an unknown addend and subtraction equations that represent the same story problem.
- Match (using words or other representations) Compare and Put Together/Take Apart, Addend Unknown problems and equations.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to solve Compare, Difference Unknown story problems and match addition and subtraction equations to the problems.

## Narrative

In previous lessons is for students to match addition and subtraction equations to Compare, Difference Unknown and Put Together/Take Apart Addend Unknown problems. This lesson builds on the previous work of this unit by asking students to find two equations that match a story problem and describe how each equation relates to the story problem. Students continue to deepen their understanding of the relationship between addition and subtraction and their understanding of subtraction as an unknown addend problem.
