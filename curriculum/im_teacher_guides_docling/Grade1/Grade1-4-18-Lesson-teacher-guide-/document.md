## Standards

Addressing

1.NBT.B.3

The purpose of this activity is for students to learn Stage 1 of a new center called Get Your Numbers in Order. Students use their understanding of relative magnitude to order numbers. Students take turns placing two-digit numbers on the board so that the numbers across the board go from least to greatest. If a number can't be placed on the gameboard, students say 'pass' and get 1 point. Then it is their partner's turn. The player with the fewest points when all the spaces on the board are filled is the winner. Students should remove the cards that show 10 before they start.

Consider laminating gameboards or placing them in sheet protectors so they can be used multiple times.

## Launch

- Groups of 2
- Give each group a set of cards and a gameboard.
- 'We are going to learn a new center called Get Your Numbers In Order.'
- Display the gameboard and number cards.
- 'When it is your turn, choose 2 cards and make a 2-digit number. Write your number in a space on the gameboard. All of the numbers across the board must be in order from least to greatest. You may not move a number once it is placed on the board. If you can't write your number on the board, you get 1 point. Take turns until all the spaces on the board are filled or you can't make any more numbers that fit on the board. The person with the fewest points when the board is filled is the winner.'
- Demonstrate playing a couple rounds with the class.
- 'Now you will play with your partner.'
