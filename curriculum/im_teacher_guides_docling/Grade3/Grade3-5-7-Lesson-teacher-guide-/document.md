## Goals

- Comprehend (in spoken language) the meaning of the terms 'numerator' and 'denominator.'
- Explain (orally and in writing) strategies for locating and labeling non-unit fractions (including fractions greater than 1) on the number line.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to locate non-unit fractions on the number line.

## Narrative

Previously, students built non-unit fractions from unit fractions, with diagrams and fraction strips. Now, students deepen their understanding of fractions on the number line as they locate and label non-unit fractions. Students also discuss how they know when fractions are less than 1 or greater than 1 and are introduced to the terms numerator and denominator .
