## Goals

- Critique (orally and in writing) a strategy for calculating an unknown side length.
- Explain (orally) strategies for calculating the area of a figure composed of rectangles, including figures with unknown side lengths.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to calculate the areas of ungridded figures made of rectangles, including figures with unknown side lengths.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students found the areas of figures that were fully gridded with squares, and moved toward figures without a grid that had all their side lengths labeled. In this lesson, students use the strategies they have learned to decompose the figures into non-overlapping rectangles. They realize that not all measurements need to be given, and that some lengths can be determined given the rectangular structure of these figures.
