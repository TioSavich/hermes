## Goals

- Create (in writing) and present a How Many Do You See? routine involving equal groups.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to apply their understanding of equal groups and multiplication to create a How Many Do You See? routine.

## Narrative

This lesson provides an opportunity to observe the ways in which students find the number of objects in equal groups. After the Warm-up, students create their own How Many Do You See? routine and then facilitate it with other students in the class.

If students need additional support with the concepts in this lesson, refer back to Unit 1, Section B in the curriculum materials.
