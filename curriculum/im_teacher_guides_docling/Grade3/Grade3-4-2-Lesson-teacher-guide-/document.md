## Goals

- Compare and contrast (orally) strategies for solving 'how many in each group?' word problems.
- Explain (using words or other representations) strategies for solving 'how many in each group?' problems.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to solve 'how many in each group?' problems in a way that makes sense to them.

## Narrative

Previously, students solved 'how many groups?' problems in a way that made sense to them. In this lesson, students extend problems involving sharing into equal groups to include 'how many in each group?' problems. Students again have the flexibility to represent and solve problems using any strategy that makes sense to them in this lesson. If students use connecting cubes, encourage them to draw a picture to match their work. At the end of this lesson, division is defined as finding the number of groups or finding the size of each group when we share into groups of equal size.
