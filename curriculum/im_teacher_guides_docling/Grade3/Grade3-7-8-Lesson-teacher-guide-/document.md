## Goals

- Compare and contrast (orally) strategies for calculating the perimeter of shapes given all the side lengths.
- Explain (using words and other representations) strategies for calculating the perimeter of a shape given its attributes and some side lengths.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to find perimeters of shapes given all or some of the side lengths.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students learned about attributes of two-dimensional shapes. They also learned about perimeter and drew shapes with a given perimeter. In this lesson, students find perimeters of shapes given side lengths and use the attributes of shapes to find the perimeter given only some of the side lengths.
