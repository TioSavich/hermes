## Goals

- Critique (orally) statements and generalizations about fractions, whole numbers represented as fractions, and fraction comparisons.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to consider statements about fractions that will help them solidify their understanding of fractions as numbers.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students learned what fractions are and how to represent fractions with area diagrams, fraction strips, and on the number line. Students also compared fractions, and recognized and generated equivalent fractions. In this lesson, students think about and discuss statements that address their understanding of important ideas about fractions.

If students need additional support with the concepts in this lesson, refer back to Unit 5, Section C in the curriculum materials.

## Student Task Statement

Discuss each statement in 3 rounds with your group.

- Round 1: Go around the group and state whether you agree, disagree, or are unsure about the statement and justify your choice. You can change your response in the next round.
- Round 2: Go around the group and state whether you agree, disagree, or are unsure about the statement you or someone else made in the first round. You can change your response in the next round.
- Round 3: State and circle the word to show whether you agree, disagree, or are unsure about the statement now that discussion has ended.

Repeat the rounds for as many statements as you can.
