## Goals

- Calculate the difference between quantities and describe (orally) comparisons, using 'more' and 'less.'
- Use and explain (orally) estimation or rounding strategies to solve a real-world problem.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to use their understanding of estimation, rounding, and addition within 1,000 to solve a problem about a class wish list.

## Narrative

This lesson is optional because it does not address any new mathematical content standards. This lesson does provide students with an opportunity to apply precursor skills of mathematical modeling (MP4).

In this lesson, students put together a wish list of supplies they would like to get for their classroom given a large collection of choices and their costs. They are given a budget and freedom to decide how to spend the money. As they make choices, students round the costs before they check the total amount they are spending. Students then compare their wish list with another group. Groups compare their wish lists and how much they spent in each category.
