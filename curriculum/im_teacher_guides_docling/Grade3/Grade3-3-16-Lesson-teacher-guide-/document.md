## Goals

- Describe (orally) a three-digit number including details based on rounding to the nearest ten or hundred.
- Determine (in writing) all the numbers that can round to a given multiple of 10 or multiple of 100, and describe (orally) observed patterns.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to use their understanding of rounding to consider all the numbers that round to a given number.

## Narrative

Students deepen their understanding of rounding to go beyond accurately rounding individual numbers as they think about what numbers round to a given number. Working backward from a multiple of 10 or a multiple of 100 allows students to think about the relative distances of numbers and the range of numbers that round to the given multiple of 10 or a multiple of 100. Students then use this understanding to write clues to help their classmates guess a mystery number. The rounded number becomes a useful way to describe the mystery number in this game.
