## Goals

- Calculate the product of 2 unit fractions.
- Interpret (orally and in writing) a diagram and an equation that represent the product of 2 unit fractions.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to represent products of unit fractions using diagrams and equations.

## Narrative

In previous lessons students represented products of unit fractions with diagrams and expressions. In this lesson students connect the diagrams and expressions, using the structure of the diagram to calculate the value of the expression. Students use the diagrams to find the value of many expressions. At the end of the lesson, they find the value of an expression, representing a product of unit fractions, without being given a diagram.
