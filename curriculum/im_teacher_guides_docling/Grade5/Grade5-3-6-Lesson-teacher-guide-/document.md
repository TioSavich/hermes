## Goals

- Determine the product of 2 non-unit fractions represented by a diagram.
- Represent a diagram with a multiplication expression involving 2 non-unit fractions and interpret (orally and in writing) the expression.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to calculate areas of rectangles where both side lengths are non-unit fractions.
