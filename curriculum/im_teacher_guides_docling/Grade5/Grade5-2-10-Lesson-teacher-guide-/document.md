The purpose of this activity is for students to find the area of rectangles with a side length that is a non-unit fraction. Students may use a variety of approaches to find the areas of the shaded region. Students may notice and use the structure of the rectangle and expressions to determine the area (MP7).

Monitor for and select students with the following approaches to share in the Activity Synthesis:

- Group the shaded pieces with a fractional area to make whole unit squares.
- Reason that each piece is a unit fraction and count the number of pieces.
- Multiply the whole number of units by the numerator of the fractional side length and divide the result by the denominator.

The approaches are sequenced from more concrete to more abstract to help students connect how the number of shaded pieces and the unit fraction relate to the area when a side length is a non-unit fraction. Aim to elicit both key mathematical ideas and a variety of student voices, especially students who haven't shared recently.
