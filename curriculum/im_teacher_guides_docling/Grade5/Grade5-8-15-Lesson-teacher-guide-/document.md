## Goals

- Create (in writing) and present an Estimation Exploration activity.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to apply their understanding of multi-digit multiplication and the standard algorithm of multiplication to create an Estimation Exploration activity.

## Narrative

This lesson offers teachers the opportunity to listen to ways in which students reason about multi-digit multiplication. After the Warm-up, students create their own Estimation Exploration activity and then facilitate their Estimation Exploration with other students in the class. Students can draw pictures or find images to use from books or other sources or use actual objects from around the classroom, as the teacher determines.
