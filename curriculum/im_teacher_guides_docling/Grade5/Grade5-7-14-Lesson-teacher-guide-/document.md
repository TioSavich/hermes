## Goals

- Use a coordinate grid to make a copy of a figure.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to use what they've learned about coordinate grids to make a copy of a figure.

## Narrative

This lesson is optional because it does not address any new mathematical content standards. This lesson does provide students with an opportunity to apply precursor skills of mathematical modeling (MP4). In previous lessons, students learned how to locate points on the coordinate grid and represent these points with ordered pairs. In this lesson, students begin by drawing a coordinate grid over a figure and determining the points that will be useful for drawing a copy of the figure. Then students use their plan to draw a copy of the figure. They describe the ways in which the original figure and the copy of the figure are the same and different.

## Standards

Addressing 5.G.A.2

The purpose of this activity is for students to use what they've learned about coordinate grids to plan how they will make a copy of a figure. As students create the coordinate grid, they will have to make decisions that will affect how they copy their figure, such as creating their grid with centimeters or inches, the scale they will use on their grids, and which points they will use to copy their figure. Students will work with a partner to plan the copy of their images, but will make their own copy of the image in the next activity. This provides an opportunity for students to make different choices during planning and see how those choices impact their work in the next activity. When students consider assumptions about information not given in a situation, they model with mathematics (MP4).

## Student Task Statement

Create a coordinate grid on grid paper. Use the points you chose in the previous activity to copy your figure.

1. How are the original figure and the copy of the figure alike?

## Student Response

Sample responses:

2. The original figure has color, but my figure is just the outline. My copy doesn't have all of the shapes from the original. I think this is because I missed some points that would be important to draw. My copy is smaller than the original. I think this is because the grid I copied on the original and the grid I used to draw are different sizes.
