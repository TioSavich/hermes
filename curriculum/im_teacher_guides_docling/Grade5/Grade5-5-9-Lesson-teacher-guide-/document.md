## Goals

- Determine the correct order of decimals and explain (orally) the strategy used.
- Identify a decimal between 2 given decimals to thousandths and justify (orally) the identification.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to order decimals.

## Narrative

In this lesson, students apply their understanding of decimal place value to list a set of decimal numbers in increasing size. Some students may wish to use a number line to help locate the numbers. Students who choose to use the number lines have the interesting task of figuring out which numbers to put at the ends of their number line, and which will require thinking carefully about place value and the size of the numbers they are locating (MP6).
