## Goals

- Describe (orally and in writing) how different, equivalent multiplication expressions represent the area of a given rectangle with fractional side lengths greater than 1.
- Explain (orally) strategies used to determine the area of a rectangle with fractional side lengths and represent the area with a multiplication expression.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to find the area of a rectangle when one of the side lengths is a fraction greater than 1.
