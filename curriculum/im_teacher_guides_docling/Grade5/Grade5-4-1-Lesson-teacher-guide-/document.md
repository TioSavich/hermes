## Goals

- Describe (orally and using other representations) strategies for multiplying a two-digit number by a one-digit number, by a two-digit number, and by a three-digit number.
- Explain (orally and in writing) strategies for estimating the product of a two-digit number and a three-digit number.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to make estimates and calculations of products.

## Narrative

In IM Grade 4, students multiplied two-digit numbers by two-digit numbers and up to four-digit numbers by one-digit numbers, using strategies based on place-value understanding and the properties of operations. Students learned a partial-products strategy for multiplication and represented it with diagrams and equations. This lesson gives teachers an opportunity to see how students apply previous work to estimate and find products. Expect to see a wide range of strategies from students, and be sure to listen to them as they explain their reasoning. In the Activity Synthesis, the strategies highlighted involve place value and the properties of operations, which will be built on in future lessons.
