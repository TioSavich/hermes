## Goals

- Interpret (orally and in writing) the steps in the standard algorithm used to multiply a three-digit number by a two-digit number, with composing new units.
- Use the standard algorithm to multiply three-digit numbers by two-digit numbers, with composing new units.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to understand and apply the standard algorithm for multiplication when multiplying three-digit numbers by two-digit numbers, with composition of new units.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students learned to use the standard algorithm for multiplication of up to five-digit numbers by one-digit numbers when a new unit is composed. They also multiplied three-digit numbers by two-digit numbers, without composing a new unit. In this lesson, students combine these two skills. They use the standard algorithm for multiplication of a three-digit number by a two-digit number and record the composition of new units.
