## Goals

- Draw the graph of a set of coordinate pairs.
- Use additive rules to determine coordinates for points in the plane.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to represent corresponding terms in two patterns on the coordinate grid.

## Narrative

The purpose of this lesson is to continue to analyze the relationship between two patterns by plotting corresponding numbers on the coordinate grid. After generating patterns in previous lessons, students now generate patterns and interpret corresponding terms as coordinates on the coordinate grid. They make these ordered pairs from the patterns and plot them on the coordinate grid. They observe patterns and interpret the meaning of points on the coordinate grid in terms of generating rules.
