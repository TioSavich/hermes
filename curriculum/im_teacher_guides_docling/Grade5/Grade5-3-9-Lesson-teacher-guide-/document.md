## Goals

- Explain (orally and in writing) strategies for finding the product of fractions or mixed numbers.
- Interpret (orally and in writing) a fraction multiplication equation that represents an area in context.

## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to apply what they have learned about fraction multiplication to solve problems.

## Narrative

In previous lessons, students developed an understanding of how to find products of fractions both with an area context and with no context. The purpose of this lesson is to use this knowledge to solve problems about different national flags. Students work with problems where the side lengths are given and they are finding the area in square units of a particular region. Students also solve problems where they are determining what fraction of the flag a certain region is. The distinction between these two types of problems is subtle, but is important as the answer to the first problem involves square units but units are not needed for the second problem.
