## Lesson Purpose

The purpose of this lesson is for students to explain how the product of two numbers compares to one factor based on the size of the other factor.

## Narrative

The goal of this optional lesson is to compare the size of a product to the size of one factor, applying what students have learned in the last several lessons. They make comparisons and describe in general how to make comparisons, using a strategy that makes sense to them. No representation is provided or suggested, which gives students an opportunity to think about the different methods they have learned, namely calculation, number lines, and the distributive property, and to choose the one that makes sense in each situation.
