## Activity Synthesis

The purpose of this discussion is both to make connections and to contrast between a number's additive and multiplicative inverse. A key idea to emphasize is that multiplicative inverses require that the numbers have the same sign in order for the product to be positive, while additive inverses must have opposite signs in order for their sum to be 0.
