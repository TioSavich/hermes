The source PDFs draw their expressions as glyph outlines rather than as text,
so the conversion carried the frame of an item list and nothing inside it.
scripts/curriculum/recover_guide_expressions.py reads those outlines and writes
the expressions back into the raw-extract block, in place, without moving a
line. The prose in these files is the conversion's; the restored expressions are
the same source, read a second way.
